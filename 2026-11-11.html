United MileagePlus — PlusPoints upgrades go dynamic in Feb 2027

Effective February 2027 · Tier 2 coverage; United’s own announcement is the primary source[9]

Beginning in February 2027, United is retiring the fixed PlusPoints upgrade chart and moving to a dynamic model, where the points required for a given upgrade will vary with route, cabin, timing, and demand. The industry consensus is that this will function as a devaluation, though United is framing it as “simplification.”[9]

if you hold PlusPoints and know your 2027 travel calendar, apply them to known high-value international upgrades on current fixed pricing before February — after the cutover the value per PlusPoint is almost certainly going down.
