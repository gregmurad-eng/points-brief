Today's Transfer Bonuses

Seven bonuses are live across the four major transferable stacks. Two of them — Citi→Turkish and Capital One→Flying Blue — close at month end and are five days from their deadline. Chase's 80% to IHG remains the headline number of the quarter.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards: 80%

Ratio 1,000 UR = 1,800 IHG  ·  Ends Dec 3, 2026 (11:59pm ET)  ·  Min 1,000 points  ·  Processing ~5 hours[1]

Chase's highest-ever bonus to IHG, confirmed by AwardWallet[1] and Frequent Miler's running table[2]. IHG One Rewards points value at best around 0.5–0.6 cents, so an 80% bonus brings the effective transfer value into the 0.9–1.1 cent range. That is strong only if you have a specific IHG redemption lined up — aspirational properties on peak dates, the Points & Cash fourth-night-free mechanic, or a Vignette collection stay you already priced in points.

Transfer only if you have a specific IHG redemption to book. IHG point values are weak enough that a speculative 80% transfer still produces a poor-value balance if you never find the right stay.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Miles & Smiles: 40%

Ratio 1,000 TY = 1,400 Turkish miles  ·  Ends Nov 30, 2026 (11:59pm Turkey time)  ·  Cap 50,000 bonus miles (max transfer 125,000 TY)  ·  Credit timing bonus posts after campaign ends[3]

The sweet spot that justifies holding Citi points for many award chasers: Turkish Miles & Smiles still prices partner business class North America→Europe at 65,000 miles one-way on Star Alliance, including United and Lufthansa[4]. With the 40%, that's roughly 46,500 Citi points plus taxes for a transatlantic business seat. Note the cap — transferring more than 125,000 TY in this window doesn't earn any additional bonus miles.

Transfer only if you have United, Lufthansa, SWISS, or Air Canada business space held or clearly identified. Turkish's own IT routinely eats transfers to a black box, and chasing space after the transfer is where this sweet spot becomes a trap.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club: 40%

Ratio 1,000 UR = 1,400 Virgin miles  ·  Ends Dec 30, 2026  ·  Start Nov 17, 2026[2]

Chase's 40% beats the concurrent Amex→Virgin Atlantic 30% bonus (which ends Nov 30) — same airline, different source. Virgin Atlantic remains the easiest route to the ANA First Class sweet spot (85k–120k Virgin miles one-way), to Air France/KLM business through the SkyTeam side of Virgin's chart, and to Delta One space Delta itself won't sell for miles. Both bonuses stacking on the same airline this month is rare alignment.

If you hold both UR and MR and need Virgin miles for a specific redemption, prefer Chase — the 40% dominates the 30% on identical mileage. Don't transfer speculatively; Virgin surcharges are heavy and the sweet spots are inventory-constrained.

Amex Membership Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy: 35%

Ratio 1,000 MR = 1,350 Bonvoy points  ·  Ends Dec 10, 2026  ·  Start Oct 20, 2026[2]

MR→Marriott is normally 1:1, so this lifts the effective rate meaningfully — but Marriott point values have drifted to around 0.73–0.75 cents[5] after this year's category bumps and the 70k-off-peak / 120k-peak band widening. Effective MR→Bonvoy value here is roughly 1.0 cent per MR, which is below MR's open-market value against most airline partners. Useful for a known Bonvoy booking; poor for topping up in general.

Transfer only to close a specific gap on a Marriott booking you've already priced. A 35% bonus on a devalued currency is still not a reason to move MR speculatively.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus Avios: 30%

Ratio 1,000 UR = 1,300 Avios  ·  Ends Dec 1, 2026  ·  Start Nov 1, 2026[2]

Routine 30% on the Avios triple, all three transferable from any of Chase, Amex, Capital One, and Bilt — and all three programs share Avios 1:1 between themselves. The two durable sweet spots: Iberia short-haul inside Europe and single-carrier American Airlines domestic short-haul (BA Avios on AA has gotten patchy; Iberia Avios on AA is more reliable). Capital One matches this at 30% to multiple Avios flavours historically; Chase's 30% is the active version right now.

Transfer only if you've already found specific Avios award space. The Avios family's short-haul sweet spots are inventory-driven; without a confirmed seat held in cash or on hold, the bonus won't close a gap it hasn't identified.

Also live and less distinctive: Capital One→Flying Blue 20% through Nov 30[6], Capital One→Copa 30% through Dec 1[2], Amex→Virgin Atlantic 30% through Nov 30 (dominated by the Chase 40%)[7], Amex→Avianca LifeMiles 10% through Dec 16, and Capital One→Qatar Avios 15% through Dec 14[2].

Transfers are one-way and irreversible. Move points only with a specific redemption in mind — ideally one you've priced or held on hold — not on bonus size alone.

Program Watch

Amex Platinum: $600 Hotel Credit requires 2-night FHR stay starting Jan 1, 2027

American Express announced on November 24, 2026 that effective January 1, 2027, Fine Hotels + Resorts bookings will require a minimum two-night stay to qualify for the Platinum Card's $600 Hotel Credit[8]. The Hotel Collection already had this requirement; FHR did not. Effect: closes the single-night FHR arbitrage that made the credit easy to clear on a one-night Vegas or airport-adjacent stay. Confirmed independently by View from the Wing[9].

If you have a one-night FHR booking planned using this credit, lock it in before Dec 31, 2026. After that, the credit becomes meaningfully harder to clear on short domestic trips.

IHG One Rewards credit card refresh: new Premier Select, higher fees on Premier and Business

Chase and IHG refreshed the IHG card portfolio in November 2026[10]: a new Premier Select card launched at a $350 annual fee, the Premier annual fee rose from $99 to $150, and the Business from $99 to $200. The fourth-night-free benefit on award stays now requires $5,000 in annual spend on some tiers. Updated earning rates (including grocery as a bonus category) take effect immediately for existing holders; higher annual fees apply at 2027 renewal.

If you hold the Premier for the free anniversary night, the $51 fee increase still clears easily against the night's point value. If you hold the Business purely for the $99 fee, model whether the new benefits clear the $200 before renewal.

American AAdvantage ended Hyatt partnership on Nov 15, 2026

American's partnership with World of Hyatt wound down on November 15, 2026[11]. The AA/Hyatt earn-across-partners link had been a quiet but useful mutual-earn setup since 2019. Effect: no more earning AAdvantage miles for Hyatt stays and no more World of Hyatt points for AA flights; existing linked accounts remain linked but no new activity cross-credits.

No action required; this only matters if you had an in-flight earning promotion tied to the partnership. Status-based Hyatt benefits are unaffected.

If you have confirmed Star Alliance business space on hold that Turkish will ticket: transfer the exact amount needed before Nov 30. Otherwise skip — Citi has run four Turkish bonuses in 2026 and another is likely inside the next 90 days.
