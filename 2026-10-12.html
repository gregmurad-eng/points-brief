American's mixed cash-and-miles booking flow launched the week of 2026-10-02 on aa.com and the AA app for domestic AA-operated routes (ex-Alaska/Hawaii).[16] Points Crowd flagged on launch that AA did not disclose the per-mile cash-equivalent value used in the mixed flow, and ten days later that remains the case. Your move: treat Cash + Miles strictly as a last-resort balance-liquidation tool, not a redemption strategy. Until AA publishes the exchange rate, assume ≤1.0¢/mile and compare any Cash + Miles screen directly to the all-miles award price at the same fare class.
