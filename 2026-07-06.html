Lead Story

Hyatt's best-kept transfer secret is on a countdown clock

For years the quiet backbone of Chase Ultimate Rewards value has been the 1:1 pipe into World of Hyatt — the rare hotel program where a point routinely buys 1.6 cents or more of real room value. That pipe is narrowing. Chase and Hyatt are moving the Sapphire Preferred transfer ratio from 1:1 to 4:3, meaning three Hyatt points for every four Ultimate Rewards points you send.

The nuance that matters: anyone approved for the card on or after June 15, 2026 already lives under the worse ratio. But existing cardholders keep 1:1 transfers until October 1, 2026. That's a roughly three-month window where the old math still holds — and it's a strong argument for topping up a Hyatt account (only when you have a specific stay in mind) before the fall.

Hyatt is sweetening the timing from its own side too: buy World of Hyatt points at a 20% bonus through July 27, and earn up to 30,000 bonus points at Hyatt Place and Hyatt Select brands on qualifying multi-night stays.

Today's Briefs

Delta's summer flash sale closes July 15

Delta SkyMiles is running an award flash sale with reduced mileage pricing to Europe, South America and the Caribbean. Windows are narrow and blackout-heavy, so search dates broadly — but for a SkyMiles balance that usually feels stuck, this is the rare moment the program pays out.

United reshuffles how miles are earned

Starting April 2, 2026, United MileagePlus members without a co-branded card earn fewer miles on United flights, and basic-economy fares stop earning entirely unless you hold status or a United card. The offset: upgrade awards are now available to all Premier members on award tickets, a genuinely useful expansion for anyone burning miles on United metal.

Marriott and United launch a dual-currency stay-and-fly offer

Book qualifying stays and flights between July 15 and September 30 and earn in both Bonvoy points and MileagePlus miles. Marriott is separately handing out 5,000 bonus points per stay at its Design Hotels collection on two-plus-night bookings.

Amex adds a Virgin Atlantic runway

The Amex → Virgin Atlantic 30% bonus runs through July 31 — Virgin's dynamic pricing can surface some of the lowest premium-cabin rates anywhere, though the carrier-imposed surcharges run high. Great for London; do the fees math before you transfer.

The Signal

If you're an existing Sapphire Preferred holder, your 1:1 Chase→Hyatt transfers survive only until October 1. Don't speculatively hoard — but if you have a Hyatt stay you'll book this year, this is the last clean window to fund it at the old ratio. Pair it with the 20%-off points-purchase bonus and you're stacking two deadlines in your favor.
