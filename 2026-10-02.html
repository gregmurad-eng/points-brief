Today's Transfer Bonuses

Six public transfer bonuses are live right now, and the clock is loud on all of them. Chase's record 70 percent to Marriott and Amex's 30 percent to Hilton both expire inside two weeks, with Citi's JAL and Avianca pushes close behind.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy · 70%

Ratio 1:1.7 · Expires October 15, 2026 at 11:59pm ET · 13 days remaining

Chase's largest-ever Marriott transfer bonus — 1,000 UR becomes 1,700 Bonvoy, unlimited transfers, with the bonus posted by Marriott within seven days of transfer[1][2]. Marriott points are valued around 0.7 cents each after the May 2026 chart devaluation, which puts the effective transferred Chase point at roughly 1.19 cents — below the ~1.5 cents you can get from redeeming UR directly through Chase Travel, and well below Hyatt-tier value.

transfer only if you already have a specific Marriott redemption booked or priced out; a round-trip at ~1.2 cents is not a reason to drain a Chase balance, but it is the right rate for a ~90,000-point Category 6 night you were already going to book this fall.

Amex Membership Rewards → Hilton Honors · 30%

Ratio 1:2.6 · Expires October 14, 2026 · 12 days remaining · may be targeted

Standard Amex-to-Hilton transfers at 1:2, elevated to 1:2.6 for this window[3][4]. Check your Amex transfer portal — this offer has been running as broadly available but Amex has flagged it as targeted for some accounts. Hilton points sit at roughly 0.5 cents each, so even with the bonus you are at ~1.3 cents per Amex point. Narrow use-case.

skip unless you have a specific Waldorf, Conrad or peak-pricing night held and the math on cash-vs-points favours the transfer; the rate is not a reason to speculatively convert MR to Hilton balances that then earn you no interest and no flexibility.

Amex Membership Rewards → Air France-KLM Flying Blue · 25%

Ratio 1:1.25 · October 1 – October 31, 2026 (ends 11:59pm MT)

Unlimited transfers, 999,999 points per transaction, must be enrolled in Flying Blue[5][6]. First Amex-to-Flying Blue bonus since March 2026 — and it lands squarely in the post-September-8 tiered-fare window, where AF/KLM direct Standard business fares effectively moved from ~60,000 miles to ~75,000 miles each way. Partner-airline pricing on Flying Blue was explicitly left unchanged at the Sept 8 rollout.

transfer for a specific partner redemption you have already pulled up on the Flying Blue award search — Kenya Airways to Africa, GOL inside Brazil, Vietnam Airlines in business — not for AF/KLM direct metal unless the Promo Reward of the month is one you actually want.

Capital One Miles → British Airways Club Avios · 20%

Ratio 1:1.2 · October 1 – October 31, 2026 · no registration, no cap

Capital One's first new transfer bonus of October, and the one you can also fire at Iberia, Aer Lingus, Finnair and Qatar via Avios combine[7][8]. Transfers from Cap One to BA are generally instant.

transfer to Iberia Avios (not BA) if your use case is Madrid short-haul AA domestic or trans-Atlantic premium — the Iberia award chart on partner space is where this bonus actually produces outsized value, and Avios combine lets you move miles family-to-family once you have your BA balance.

Citi ThankYou → Japan Airlines Mileage Bank · 30%

Ratio 1:1.3 (annual-fee cards) · 1:0.91 (no-fee cards) · Expires October 24, 2026 at 11:59pm ET

Citi's two-week-old JAL launch promo is now 22 days from expiry[9][10]. With the bonus, 43,000 Citi points produces ~55,900 JAL miles — enough for one-way North America to Japan in business class on JAL metal (55,000 miles).

transfer only if you hold a Citi Strata Premier, Strata Elite, Prestige or AT&T Access More and have confirmed JAL business-class award space on jal.co.jp before you move points; JAL Mileage Bank restricts new accounts from redemption for 60 days, so a brand-new JAL account cannot act on this bonus in time.

Citi ThankYou → Avianca LifeMiles · 25%

Ratio 1:1.25 (annual-fee cards) · 1:0.875 (no-fee cards) · Expires October 24, 2026 at 11:59pm ET

Minimum 1,000 points, instant transfer, same card-tier split as the JAL offer[11][12]. LifeMiles continues to be one of the best Star Alliance tools reachable from a US bank — no fuel surcharges, distance-based pricing, 63,000 miles one-way US to Europe in business.

transfer if you have a US-to-Europe or short-haul US-domestic Star Alliance award on hold with Avianca; LifeMiles rarely rewards speculative balances because the program periodically devalues sweet spots with little notice.

Rove Miles → JAL / Flying Blue / Qatar Avios · 50% / 40% / up to 35%

Three bonuses, all expire October 31, 2026 at 11:59pm EDT

Rove launched JAL as a 1:1 transfer partner on October 1 alongside a 50 percent bonus (1,000 Rove → 1,500 JAL), extended its Flying Blue 40 percent, and ran up to 35 percent on Qatar Privilege Club Avios[13][14]. Rove is a younger program with a thinner earn stack than Amex or Chase, but these launch rates are competitive and the JAL route especially is notable because it undercuts Citi's 1:1.3 and Capital One's 1:1 on raw transfer efficiency.

use only if you already hold a meaningful Rove balance — do not open a Rove account mid-October to chase these rates, because the acquisition economics will not catch up to a one-time launch promo.

Program Watch

Bilt Rewards → World of Hyatt devalues to 4:3 on January 1, 2027

Announced October 1, 2026 · effective January 1, 2027 · ALL Bilt members

Bilt quietly posted the devaluation to its Hyatt transfers page yesterday[15][16][17]. From January 1, 10,000 Bilt points will produce 7,500 Hyatt points — a 25 percent cut mirroring the Chase change that landed the same day. This closes the last 1:1 flexible-points route to Hyatt that was open to every cardholder. The only remaining 1:1 route from a transferable-points program is Chase Ultimate Rewards from the Sapphire Reserve or Sapphire Reserve for Business, which carve the Hyatt lane as a premium-tier benefit.

if you are a Bilt holder with a specific 2027 Hyatt stay in mind, pull the transfer before December 31 — but only if that stay is actually priced and the dates are inside Hyatt's bookable window; speculative Hyatt balances are now worse insurance than they were a month ago because category creep continues on the back end.

Chase UR → Hyatt 4:3 cut landed yesterday on Sapphire Preferred and Ink Preferred

Effective October 1, 2026 · existing cardholders · Sapphire Reserve and Sapphire Reserve for Business retain 1:1

The 110-day-advance-notice cut announced in June arrived on schedule[18][19]. No grace period, no walk-back — the ledger logged this as HIT-pending (call 2026-09-18-1). Chase has now explicitly segmented Hyatt transfer value as a Sapphire-Reserve-tier benefit, which is the first time Hyatt has sat behind a premium-card paywall from any US bank.

if you only hold the Sapphire Preferred or Ink Preferred, Hyatt is no longer a reason to transfer UR — Marriott at the current 70 percent bonus is the better math on Chase hotel transfers today, but see the Chase-Marriott caution above.

Rove Miles added JAL Mileage Bank as a 1:1 transfer partner on October 1

Launch day October 1, 2026 · structural · follows Citi's September 20 JAL add

Second major-US-bank-adjacent program to add JAL as a direct transfer partner inside two weeks, after Citi[13][20]. Capital One is the third US option (added 2022). This is a genuine shift in the JAL transfer landscape — a partner that had three US paths in 2024 (Marriott, Capital One, indirect) now has four direct options alongside Marriott, with a competitive bonus arms race already underway.

no action today; this matters for the next North America to Tokyo in business planning cycle, where you now have four source balances to consider instead of two.

Today's Play

The Hyatt lock-in window — but only if you have a stay in hand

Both Chase (yesterday) and Bilt (announced yesterday, effective January 1) have now devalued their Hyatt transfer ratios for the broad cardholder base. For Bilt specifically, you have 90 days to move points at 1:1 before the 4:3 ratio takes effect. The trade coverage is already pushing "lock in" messaging[16], and the reflex to transfer speculative Bilt balances to Hyatt now is going to be strong.

Resist it. Hyatt points are the most valuable per-point hotel currency in the transferable stack, which also means they are the most exposed to flash category changes on specific properties — Marriott-style per-hotel creep that has been documented on a near-monthly cadence since the May 2026 chart devaluation. Speculative Hyatt balances today are worth less than they look because the back-end chart is still shifting under your feet.

transfer Bilt-to-Hyatt before December 31 only for a specific stay you have priced out — a confirmed Category 6 or 7 night, a Milestone Reward you plan to use within 12 months, a known-dates aspirational booking. For everything else, hold Bilt as flexible points. Transferring at 1:1 to lock in a rate you may never redeem is still worse than transferring at 4:3 for a redemption you actually book.
