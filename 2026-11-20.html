United Premier status 2027: PQP-only, 1K raised to 32,000[9]

Announced October 13: United eliminates the combined Premier qualifying flights + PQP route for 2027. The PQP-only thresholds carry forward at 6,000 Silver / 12,000 Gold / 18,000 Platinum, but Premier 1K moves from 28,000 to 32,000 PQP (a ~14% raise on the top tier)[9]. If you were coasting to 1K on flight count this year, that path is closed in 2027 — plan for 32,000 PQP or retarget Platinum. If you hold a United co-brand, factor the January starter PQP into the gap you still need to close.

Hyatt five-tier award chart six months in: sweet spots largely intact[10]

The May 20 overhaul moved Hyatt from three tiers (Off-Peak / Standard / Peak) to five (Lowest / Low / Moderate / Upper / Top), with 136 hotels reclassified (112 up, 24 down). Fresh data as of November 2026 shows average points costs rose but median prices stayed flat, and categories 3–7 saw no material change — the pain is concentrated in Category 8 (now 35,000–75,000 points per night, versus the old 35,000–45,000)[10]. TPG's November valuation holds World of Hyatt points at 1.65¢[11].

Hyatt remains the single best use of Chase UR — just verify the specific hotel-night-category on the new chart before transferring. The Milan Park Hyatt, Ventana Big Sur, and category-8 overwater villas are where the devaluation actually bites.

Delta SkyMiles continued slide: TPG cuts valuation to 1.0¢[11]

The Points Guy's November 2026 valuations dropped Delta SkyMiles to 1.0 cent per mile, down from 1.2¢ — about 17% lower, with award prices up 37% year-over-year on routes they sample versus 11% on cash fares[11]. Do not stockpile SkyMiles. Transfer Amex MR or Capital One miles to SkyMiles only against a priced-out ticket at the moment of booking, and treat any speculative "I might fly Delta next year" balance as cash-equivalent at best.
