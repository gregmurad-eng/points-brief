Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards · 80%

Chase's headline bonus of the quarter is still live. One Ultimate Rewards point becomes 1.8 IHG points on transfer, through December 3, 2026. This is the largest Chase→IHG bonus of 2026, and it follows a 70% run in August — Chase has now stepped up, not down, which is the opposite of what the late-summer pattern suggested.[1][2][3]

Amex Membership Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy · 35%

Amex's Marriott bonus runs through December 10, 2026. One MR point becomes 1.35 Marriott points. Marriott transfers at a 1:1 base from Amex, and 35% is the typical high-end of the Amex→Marriott range — Amex did not run an Amex→Marriott bonus at 25% or greater from February through October this year, so this is the live window.[1][2][4][5]

Marriott prices awards dynamically with no cancellation grace on transferred points — if you cancel the booking, points stay in Bonvoy, not Amex. There is no un-transfer.

move points only against a Marriott booking you have already placed on hold with cash-rate confirmation. Treat this as a "fund a specific redemption" bonus, not a stockpiling bonus — Marriott's variable pricing eats speculative transfers.

Amex Membership Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club · 30%

Expires November 30, 2026 — nine days away, the nearest-dated bonus in play. One MR point becomes 1.3 Virgin Atlantic miles.[1][2] Virgin Atlantic is the strongest use of Membership Rewards right now for partner awards — notably ANA first-class trans-Pacific (where Virgin charges a fraction of what ANA's own Mileage Club asks) and Delta One from the US to Europe via SkyTeam partners.

transfer only if you have already confirmed partner award space on a specific date — either via the partner airline's own website, ExpertFlyer, or a Virgin agent holding the seat. Do not transfer "in case" Virgin releases space later; it rarely does.
