Two best-ever-matching transfer bonuses are entering their final week: Chase to Marriott at 70% through October 15, and Amex to Hilton at 30% through October 14. Act only where you already know what night you are booking.

Today’s Transfer Bonuses

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy — 70%[1][4]

1,000 Chase → 1,700 Marriott · expires 11:59 PM ET October 15, 2026 · no minimum · unlimited transfers · Marriott credits the bonus within 7 days of transfer

This ties the all-time high for Chase to Marriott, which has only run at 70% once before. Base ratio is 1:1. The economics only work for specific redemptions — Marriott nightly rates run 60k to 120k on the top end under dynamic pricing, so this bonus is a tool for someone already looking at a 100k-plus award night, not a reason to generate one.[6]

Your move: Transfer only if you already have a specific Marriott award night priced out at 50k Bonvoy or higher and have confirmed availability on the dates you need. If you don’t have a stay identified by Monday, let the bonus pass — this corridor runs again, typically every 4 to 6 months.

Amex Membership Rewards → Hilton Honors — 30%[2][4]

1,000 MR → 2,600 Hilton · expires 11:59 PM MT October 14, 2026 · base ratio 1:2 · broadly offered but confirm the promo appears on your transfer screen

Six days left. The all-in rate of 2.6 Hilton per MR beats the Amex to Marriott ratio and makes high-end Hilton properties (Waldorf, Conrad, LXR) more plausibly accessible via points. The usual trap: a Standard Room at a top Hilton can price above 150k Hilton nightly, so this bonus moves the needle only when you have a real stay in mind.[8]

Your move: Pull up Hilton’s points calendar for the hotel and dates you want BEFORE transferring; Hilton is not a transferrable-points program — once it is in Hilton it stays there. If you can’t confirm points-rate availability, don’t transfer. If you can, this is a strong rate.

Amex Membership Rewards → Flying Blue — 25%[3][4]

1,000 MR → 1,250 Flying Blue miles · expires 11:59 PM MT October 31, 2026 · base ratio 1:1

Flying Blue runs Amex bonuses more often than any other partner — this is a standard-cadence 25%, not a rare peak. Flying Blue is the correct tool for a specific class of redemptions (Promo Rewards, Delta One via SkyTeam partners, intra-Europe cash-plus-miles), and useless for most domestic routes.

Your move: Wait unless you have an active Promo Reward in your account for a route you’re actually taking. Another 25%-plus Flying Blue bonus will almost certainly appear by December.

Amex Membership Rewards → Aeromexico Rewards — 20%[4]

1,000 MR → 1,920 Aeromexico points · expires October 30, 2026 · base ratio 1:1.6

Niche. Aeromexico is useful for one-way Delta partner awards and Mexico City positioning flights — outside those, there are better uses for an MR point. Note Aeromexico award pricing has itself been drifting dynamic over the past year, so a 20% transfer bonus doesn’t rescue a bad underlying rate.

Your move: No action unless you’ve already priced a specific Aeromexico award at a decent rate and need the top-up miles. Do not speculate.

Capital One Miles → British Airways Avios — 20%[4]

100 Cap One → 120 BA Avios · expires October 31, 2026 · base ratio 1:1

BA Avios pools with Iberia, Aer Lingus, and Qatar, which gives this bonus more surface area than it looks. Best use cases: short-haul American Airlines domestic awards (still cheap at the BA chart), Iberia economy TATL, Aer Lingus to Dublin.

Your move: If you were already going to transfer Cap One miles to Avios this quarter, do it before 10/31. Don’t speculate — Avios bonuses recur every 2 to 3 months.

Citi ThankYou → JAL Mileage Bank — 30%  ·  Citi ThankYou → Avianca LifeMiles — 25%[4][7]

JAL: 1,000 TY → 1,300 JAL miles · LifeMiles: 1,000 TY → 1,250 LifeMiles · both expire October 24, 2026 · both require a premium Citi card (Strata Elite, Strata Premier, Prestige, or AT&T Access More) for the full ratio

The JAL bonus is the more interesting of the two: JAL still publishes a flat partner award chart that is one of the best remaining values for first and business class to Asia on oneworld partners. LifeMiles is a solid Star Alliance currency with occasional sweet spots to Europe.

Your move: For JAL — transfer only if you have a specific partner award (Cathay, Qatar, American) priced out and held. For LifeMiles — wait; LifeMiles bonuses are among the most frequent on Citi’s calendar and 25% is below their 30-plus% peaks.

Program Watch

Chase → Hyatt transfer ratio now 4:3 for all existing Sapphire Preferred and Ink Preferred cardholders[5]

Effective October 1, 2026 · Sapphire Reserve and Sapphire Reserve for Business retain 1:1

The devaluation that was announced in June for new applicants has now landed on existing cardholders. 1,000 Chase points now moves 750 Hyatt points instead of 1,000. On a 20,000-point Hyatt night, that is 27,000 Chase points instead of 20,000 — a 35% real-cost increase. If Hyatt is your primary hotel partner, this is the strongest argument this year for upgrading to Sapphire Reserve or shifting new spend to a non-Chase transferable currency.

Your move: If you hold Sapphire Preferred or Ink Preferred AND you have a Hyatt stay you’ll book in the next 30 days, run the math on both ratios before transferring any more Chase points to Hyatt — it may now be cheaper to pay cash at a non-category property or redeem Hyatt points you already have on hand.

Announced September 29, 2026 · effective date TBD in 2027 · part of a broader Atmos partnership expansion

Your move: No action yet. If you were about to apply for a BofA Premium Rewards card for the welcome bonus, you can proceed — the transfer partnership is upside not downside — but do not switch your primary card out of a Chase or Amex ecosystem for a 2027 announcement.

Today’s Play

Both bonuses expire inside a week.
