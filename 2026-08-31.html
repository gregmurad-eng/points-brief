Today's Transfer Bonuses

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards · 70%

Ratio 1:1.7 · Ends 2026-08-31 · No stated cap[1][2][7]

Chase stepped this down from a 100% early-bird (Jul 15–30) to 70% for August. IHG frequently sells points directly at 0.5¢ each — meaning 2 IHG points per cent cash — and Chase UR at 1.7:1 is worth ~1.7¢ per UR point in IHG hands. Every serious desk that has covered this promo has told readers to skip it[7], and they are right: even with the boost, UR-to-IHG is one of the weakest uses of a UR point.

Your move: transfer only if you have already priced a specific IHG award, the property is one where paid vs points is favourable (typically top-tier resorts on peak dates), and you'd otherwise be short on IHG. If any of those is unclear, skip it and let the points sit.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Miles & Smiles · 40%

Ratio 1:1.4 · Ends 2026-08-31 (23:59 Türkiye time, UTC+3) · Cap 50,000 bonus miles · No registration[6]

Bonus is applied on Turkish's side, not Citi's — it posts after the campaign ends, so what lands in your Citi transfer confirmation is 1:1[6]. Turkish's region-to-region partner chart still prices North America ↔ Europe business at 65,000 miles one-way on Star partners including United and Lufthansa, and Miles & Smiles left that chart alone in its December 2025 domestic devaluation. This is the third Citi → Turkish 40% promo of 2026 — a well-established quarterly cadence.

Your move: transfer only if you have already confirmed Star Alliance partner award space on Miles & Smiles' calendar for a route the 65k chart actually covers. Speculative transfers into a program whose customer service is famously slow is how points die in balances.

Capital One miles → Avianca LifeMiles · 15%

Ratio 1:1.15 · Ends 2026-08-31 · Min transfer 1,000 miles · No stated cap[1][8]

Distance-based Star Alliance pricing keeps LifeMiles genuinely useful: 25,000 miles for domestic short-haul business, 63,000 for US → Europe business on Star partners, with no fuel surcharges. 15% is the low end of Capital One → LifeMiles bonuses — 20–25% has been common when Capital One wants to move volume. If the redemption isn't specific and imminent, waiting a quarter for the next bonus is not a bad bet.

Your move: transfer only if you have already priced a specific LifeMiles award and the alternative programs (Aeroplan, Air Canada, United) are more expensive for the same seat. Otherwise wait — Capital One's cadence suggests another LifeMiles bonus in Q4, and a Q4 rate at 20%+ would be materially better than tonight's 15%.

Amex Membership Rewards → British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus Avios · 30%

Ratio 1:1.3 · Aug 3 – Sept 27, 2026 · All three Avios programs[5]

Because Avios move freely between BA, Iberia, Aer Lingus, Finnair Plus, and Qatar Privilege Club at no cost, the 30% bonus effectively opens access to all five[5]. Iberia Plus off-peak short-haul to Madrid (from ~11,000 Avios one-way, or ~8,462 MR after the bonus) and BA short-haul on partner American Airlines (7,500–13,000 Avios each way) remain the two sweetest spots reachable directly from MR. 27 days left — plenty of time to plan without rushing.

Your move: before transferring, log into BA.com and confirm the exact partner award seat is available on your dates. The Avios family is instant, but the awards you actually want may not be — check first, transfer second.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Air Canada Aeroplan · 20% (30% for Aeroplan cardholders)

Ratio 1:1.2 (1:1.3 stacked for Aeroplan Visa holders on 50k+ transfers) · Aug 12 – Sept 30, 2026 · Min transfer 1,000 pts[9]

Aeroplan is Chase's most useful airline transfer partner and this is the first UR → Aeroplan bonus of 2026[9]. The Aeroplan Visa cardmember stack (an extra 10% on transfers of 50,000+ UR in a single hit, capped at 25,000 bonus Aeroplan per calendar year) gets you to an effective 30% — meaningfully better than the 20% headline. Aeroplan's dynamic-priced calendar means partner sweet spots (SAS Business to Scandinavia, ANA to Japan) still exist but need finding.

Your move: if you hold the Aeroplan Visa and can find partner award space you actually want, transfer in 50k chunks to lock the cardmember 10%. If you don't hold the card, 20% is a fair rate but no reason to speculate — Chase's Aeroplan bonuses run at 20–30% roughly annually.

Program Watch

Aeroplan · Blackstone/La Caisse 25% minority equity investment settled

Announced Aug 11, settled Aug 17: a Blackstone-led investor group (with La Caisse, PSP, and BCI) took a 25% non-controlling stake in Aeroplan Inc. at a $10B valuation, with Air Canada retaining 75% ownership and — per the release — "full operational control" over strategy and day-to-day management[10][11]. Air Canada also holds a buyback right on the minority stake between years 5 and 8. No award-chart, transfer-ratio, or earning change has been announced with the transaction. The loud consensus this week has been "PE arrives, members lose" — that call is testable and I have logged it (Ledger 2026-08-30-1); the contrarian bet is that programmatic effects of PE minority investments take multi-year timelines, not first-quarter announcements.

Atmos Rewards · Alaska + Hawaiian fully merged, still not a bank transfer partner

Today's Play

Three bonuses die tonight, and none of them is a slam-dunk transfer target on its own. The rule that keeps points from becoming dead balances is the same one that ends every offer in this brief: transfer only when the award is confirmed available on your dates. Chase → IHG at 70% is still worse per UR point than most alternative UR uses; Citi → Turkish at 40% is only good if you've verified partner space on Miles & Smiles (a program whose award search is unreliable and whose customer service is famously slow); Cap One → Avianca at 15% is the low end of the LifeMiles bonus range and Q4 typically brings a better one.

The single best move for most readers tonight is no move. If you must act, the Amex → Avios 30% (through Sept 27) and the Chase → Aeroplan 20% stacked with the Aeroplan Visa's 10% (through Sept 30) both survive tonight and give you weeks to find the right seat before transferring. Speculating into a balance you'll figure out later is how points quietly disappear.
