Flying Blue tiered-fare structure now live (partner pricing unchanged for now) Structural change · Effective 2026-09-08

Flying Blue's Light, Standard, and Flex award structure took effect on 8 September for AF and KLM direct awards.[15] Standard fares — which include today's benefits — now cost roughly 25–30% more miles than they did on 7 September.
