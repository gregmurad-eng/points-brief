Today's Play

The IHG 70% Is Doing the Heavy Lifting

Of everything on the board, the Chase → IHG 70% is the one with a defensible transfer thesis even before you find a specific booking. IHG's Points & Cash option and the 4th-night-free perk for Premier cardholders both amplify the base value, and top-tier InterContinental / Regent / Six Senses properties frequently price at 70k–100k points a night — a rate the 70% bonus turns into 42k–59k UR.

The Turkish 40% is the highest-ceiling play if you have Star Alliance business-class needs and the patience for a phone booking. The Amex → Avios 30% is the safest small-batch transfer since the currency is broadly useful across three programs.

Skip the transfer if you don't have a live award in your cart. All three programs devalue faster than UR/MR/TY.

Caution: transfers are one-way and irreversible. Never speculatively transfer points to a partner program; always confirm exact award availability and taxes for a specific date before pulling the trigger.
