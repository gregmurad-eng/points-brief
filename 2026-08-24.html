Today's Transfer Bonuses

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards · 70%

Expires August 31, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 UR → 1,700 IHG[1][2]

Chase's headline hotel promo of the summer is now in its final week. IHG award nights price at 17,000–70,000 per night at properties that actually earn the transfer, so 10,000 UR points can buy a mid-tier IHG night if the property cooperates. This has already been stepped down once — 100% July 15-30, then 70% — so a further extension would break Chase's own signalled pattern.

Only transfer if you already have a specific IHG stay in mind and the property price at award is materially cheaper than paid; do NOT speculatively park UR in IHG "for later" — IHG points expire after 12 months of no activity and have no upside from being held.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Airlines Miles & Smiles · 40%

Expires August 30, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 TYP → 1,400 Turkish[2][3]

The most valuable transfer bonus running right now for a US points holder. Turkish partner business class North America → Europe still starts at 45,000 miles one-way on Star Alliance metal at the region-to-region chart; at 1:1.4 that's ~32,000 TYP for a business seat if award space exists. The 40% is the third Citi→Turkish push of 2026 (see cadence call 2026-08-18-1 in the ledger).

Search for actual Star Alliance business availability on your dates BEFORE transferring — Turkish's own award engine is notoriously balky, and there is no refund if you park points and later can't find seats. If you have live award space held, transfer today; if you don't, wait.

Amex Membership Rewards → BA / Iberia / Aer Lingus Avios · 30%

Expires September 27, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 MR → 1,300 Avios[1][2]

Same 30% offer routed through all three Avios programs — points pool with each other, so you can transfer to whichever program's engine finds the seat. The sweet spots that still matter: Iberia short-haul into Madrid, BA Avios on partner AA short-haul North America, Aer Lingus for Dublin routings. Fuel surcharges are real on BA metal — avoid them on transatlantic.

This bonus has 34 days left; no reason to move points today unless you're within 48 hours of ticketing. The 30% will almost certainly not step up, but you gain nothing from an earlier transfer and lose flexibility.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Air Canada Aeroplan · 20%

Expires September 30, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 UR → 1,200 Aeroplan[1][2]

Aeroplan's own award chart has taxes-and-fees pain on partner premium cabins but genuine value on shorter Star Alliance routes: Vancouver–Tokyo in business at 75,000 miles is the poster child. At 1:1.2 that becomes 62,500 UR points. Aeroplan is one of the few Chase airline partners with a published, honest chart.

If you're planning a Star Alliance redemption between now and mid-2027 and already have candidate award space identified, transfer for the 20%. If not, skip — Aeroplan miles have no earning path outside transfers and paid flights.

Capital One Miles → Avianca LifeMiles · 15%

Expires August 31, 2026 · Ratio 100 Cap One → 115 LifeMiles · minimum 1,000 point transfer[1][2]

Modest bonus but LifeMiles still runs distance-based Star Alliance pricing (25,000 domestic short-haul business, 63,000 US→Europe business) and — critically — no fuel surcharges on partner metal. That's the reason to hold Cap One miles. Q4 is Capital One's most active promo window historically, which frames this as "if you need to move now" money.

If you don't have a redemption ready, wait — the ledger's open call (2026-08-23-1) forecasts a 20%+ Cap One→LifeMiles bonus between Oct 1 and Dec 31, and a 5-point bump matters at scale.

Multi-bank → Cathay Pacific Asia Miles · 10%

Expires August 28, 2026 · Ratios vary (Amex 1,000:880 · most others 1,000:1,100)[2]

Amex → Cathay is a 4:5 base ratio which, even with the 10% bump, remains a net LOSS versus most other Amex airline partners — the 1,000:880 delivered miles is not a typo. Bilt, Brex, Capital One, Citi, and Wells Fargo all transfer 1:1 to Asia Miles, so with the bonus they land at 1,000:1,100. Cathay's own metal into Hong Kong from the US at 85,000–100,000 in business is the target redemption.

If transferring from Amex MR: skip. From any other bank: only transfer if you already have Cathay business award space on hold — Cathay releases award space in bursts, and this window closes in four days.

Program Watch

Chase Sapphire Preferred → Hyatt cuts to 4:3 on October 1

Effective 2026-10-01 · Grace period ends 2026-09-30 for accounts opened before 2026-06-15[4][5]

This is the single biggest structural change in the transferable-points landscape this year. Chase Sapphire Preferred → World of Hyatt drops from 1:1 to 4:3, meaning 100,000 UR points become 75,000 Hyatt points. Cardholders who opened on or after June 15, 2026 are already on the new ratio; those who opened before get a grace period through September 30. Sapphire Reserve and Reserve for Business keep 1:1. A 25,000-point Hyatt night now costs ~33,333 UR from a Preferred. This is a real, permanent devaluation for a specific card, and the 37-day countdown is running.

American AAdvantage complimentary upgrade changes on transcon and Hawaii routes

Effective 2026-08-25 (tomorrow)[6]

Part of the AAdvantage midyear 2026 update. Upgrade eligibility and priority mechanics change on transcon (JFK/LAX/SFO/BOS) and Hawaii routes starting tomorrow. AA has framed this as a member-benefits refresh alongside the arrival of Cash + Miles bookings and new Loyalty Point Rewards menu items. Whether it's net-positive or net-negative depends on your elite tier — Executive Platinum keeps the strongest position.

United MileagePlus dynamic drift on Europe business awards

Ongoing through H1 2026 · No published chart[7]

Not a formal devaluation — United has no published chart — but Simple Flying's mid-2026 analysis notes United business one-way to Europe has drifted from ~88,000 miles to often above 110,000 miles at peak dates. Because there is no chart change to announce, this only gets caught by users pricing awards; treat it as ambient risk on any speculative United transfer.

Today's Play

The single call today: the Chase Sapphire Preferred → Hyatt grace period. If you hold a Sapphire Preferred opened before 2026-06-15, the 1:1 transfer to Hyatt disappears on October 1. Look at your Hyatt travel plans through spring 2027.
