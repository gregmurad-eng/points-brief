Chase Ultimate Rewards → Avios (BA, Iberia, Aer Lingus) — 30%

Expires Dec 1, 2026 · Base ratio 1:1 · Promo ratio 1:1.3[6]

1,000 UR becomes 1,300 Avios across any of the three Avios-member programs (BA, Iberia, Aer Lingus). Avios pool between British Airways Club, Iberia Club, Finnair, Aer Lingus AerClub and Qatar, so the destination program is largely a tax question — Iberia often avoids UK Air Passenger Duty on transatlantic routes[6].

Your move: only act if you've already priced an Avios award (transatlantic economy via Iberia, short-haul US flights on American operated by AA/Alaska via BA, or Qatar Qsuite) and know the mileage requirement. The 30% is good; the redemption is what makes it worth doing.

Amex Membership Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy — 35%

Amex Membership Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club — 30%

Expires Nov 30, 2026 · Base ratio 1:1 · Promo ratio 1:1.3[7]

1,000 MR becomes 1,300 Virgin Points. Virgin Atlantic is one of the few sweet spots where Amex MR transfers at 1.3 to useful redemptions — ANA first/business class awards via Virgin's partner chart, Delta One from the US to London/Europe (when Delta releases Virgin availability), and Air France/KLM awards[7].

Your move: worth the transfer only if you already have a Virgin Atlantic award held — ANA availability in particular is notoriously thin and must be confirmed with a Virgin phone agent before you move points.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles — 40%

Expires Nov 30, 2026 · Base ratio 1:1 · Promo ratio 1:1.4 · Bonus capped at 50,000 Turkish miles per member[8]

1,000 Citi points becomes 1,400 Turkish miles — but the bonus is capped at 50,000 Turkish miles, meaning only the first 125,000 Citi points transferred earn the bonus. Your move: good value for the long-standing Turkish sweet spots (US-Hawaii on United at 15k one-way, transatlantic business via Star Alliance partners). Only transfer if you've already called Turkish to confirm partner award space — their website search is unreliable.

Capital One Miles → Copa Airlines ConnectMiles — 30% (launch)

Expires Dec 1, 2026 · Base ratio 2:1.5 (1,000:750) · Promo ratio 1,000:975 · Min 1,000 miles[11]

Copa is a new Capital One transfer partner — added November 2, 2026 with this launch bonus. Note the base transfer ratio: 2:1.5, i.e. 1,000 Capital One miles becomes only 750 ConnectMiles normally. Amex Canada announced on November 10, 2026 that both existing Marriott Bonvoy Amex cards will see significant changes effective February 9, 2027: (a) complimentary Gold Elite status replaces Silver on both cards, (b) the free night certificate will require $3,000 in annual card spend (previously automatic), and (c) the personal card annual fee rises from CAD $120 to CAD $150[12]. Your move: Canadian cardholders — model your 2027 Marriott spend before Feb 9 to confirm whether $3,000 is realistic; if not, the free night cert is no longer a baseline benefit. US cardholders — no action required, but take the pattern as early notice.
