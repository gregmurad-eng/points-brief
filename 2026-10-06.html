Today's Transfer Bonuses

Confirmed against program and issuer pages, sorted by expiry. Transfers are one-way. Only move points with a specific award in hand.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy · 70%[2]

Expires: October 15, 2026 at 11:59pm ET · Ratio: 1,000 UR → 1,700 Marriott Bonvoy. Matches the highest Chase→Marriott bonus seen since early 2021[2]. Nine days left.

Marriott points are worth roughly 0.73¢ apiece in current secondary-market valuations[2], so the effective floor is ~1.24¢ per UR point transferred. That underperforms direct Chase redemptions through Chase Travel for most Sapphire tier cards, and it underperforms a Hyatt redemption at a cap-and-floor property — which is exactly the knife this bonus is pointed at, now that Chase→Hyatt dropped to 4:3 for pre-June-2026 Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred cardholders on October 1[6].

Only transfer if you already hold or can hold a specific Marriott redemption — a five-night cert cash-out, a peak-rate aspirational property, or a Hoshinoya-tier Asia property where Marriott still prices below cash value. Chase→Hyatt remains the better long-horizon destination for most readers whose transfer ratio is still 1:1; moving UR to Marriott speculatively now ties you to a chain that pushed award pricing up 5–10% across the portfolio this year[4].

Amex Membership Rewards → Hilton Honors · 30%[3]

Expires: October 14, 2026 · Ratio: 1,000 MR → 2,600 Hilton Honors (base 1:2, boosted to 1:2.6)[3]. Targeted — log into Amex and check "Transfer Points" to confirm you have it[3]. Eight days left.

At Hilton's typical ~0.5¢-per-point valuation, 1,000 MR converts to roughly 1.3¢ of Hilton value. That's below where MR usually earns its keep, so this is a top-off play, not a bulk transfer. The structural asymmetry still bites: MR to Hilton is Amex's lowest-floor hotel transfer, and the 30% doesn't change that.

Transfer only to top off a specific Hilton redemption you already have award space on — a Conrad Maldives night at the 150k ceiling, a Waldorf New York at 95k, or a Points & Money mix where you need a precise points balance. Do not bulk-move MR to Hilton for storage; MR is more valuable in nearly every other partner.

Citi ThankYou → Japan Airlines Mileage Bank · 30%[5]

Expires: October 24, 2026 · Ratio: Eligible annual-fee Citi ThankYou cards transfer 1,000 TY → 1,300 JAL miles. $0-annual-fee cards transfer 1,000 TY → 910 JAL miles (base 1:0.7)[5]. The post-bonus annual-fee rate drops back to 1:1[5].

This is the standout offer of the month. JAL's own award chart prices Tokyo→Honolulu business class from 43,000 miles one-way at off-peak dates[5] — with the 30% bonus, that redemption prices at ~33,100 ThankYou points on an annual-fee card. Oneworld business class to Japan at a bank-points cost that undercuts Alaska. The partnership itself only launched in September; this is the inaugural promo window.

If you hold Premier, Prestige, or Strata Premier and you already have JAL award space held — or you can see space on the JAL calendar for a specific peak-season date — transfer the exact amount for the redemption. Do NOT park ThankYou points speculatively in JAL: JAL mileage expires 36 months after each posting, and there is no partner-award reinstatement path if you cancel after an availability change. $0-annual-fee TY holders should skip this entirely; even at 1:0.91 the ratio destroys value.

Amex Membership Rewards → Flying Blue · 25%[7]

Expires: October 31, 2026 · Ratio: 1,000 MR → 1,250 Flying Blue (base 1:1, boosted to 1:1.25)[7]. 25 days left.

Flying Blue's tiered-fare rollout went live September 8, 2026, and the system has now run a full cycle of monthly Promo Rewards[7] — enough data to tell whether this partnership is still worth bank-points cost. Early reads suggest short-haul intra-Europe deals held up (Promo Rewards from 10,000 miles continue to appear monthly), but long-haul premium cabin pricing is now unambiguously dynamic. Treat Flying Blue as a short-haul fuel tank, not a J-class engine.

Transfer only when you can see a specific redemption today — a Promo Rewards short-haul for a European trip you already have dates for, or an intra-Africa flight on Kenya Airways where Flying Blue still prints below cash. Do not pre-fund Flying Blue for a hypothetical business-class redemption; the tiered-fare system makes that a worse bet now than it was in Q1.

Capital One Miles → British Airways Avios · 20%[8]

Expires: October 31, 2026 · Ratio: 1,000 Capital One → 1,200 BA Avios (base 1:1)[8]. Transfer is near-instant; no registration[8].

BA Avios distance-based short-haul redemptions — American Airlines coast-to-coast from 11,000 Avios off-peak, or AA regional hops from 8,250 Avios one-way — remain the only reliably underpriced product in the Avios system. BA transatlantic business class continues to carry severe surcharges (£500+ per leg from London), so this bonus is a US-domestic-via-Avios play, not a J-class play.

Transfer only if you need to top off for a specific American Airlines short-haul redemption you can see available on BA's search engine today. The Capital One→Qantas route (same partner family, no surcharges on partner redemptions out of Hawaii) is a stronger structural pick for the same miles, and this bonus doesn't bridge that gap.

Program Watch

Bilt Rewards → World of Hyatt drops to 4:3 on January 1, 2027[10]

Bilt quietly posted a notice on its Hyatt transfers page on October 1, 2026: the 1:1 Bilt→Hyatt ratio drops to 4:3 effective January 1, 2027, for all members and all cardholders regardless of elite status[10]. 750 Hyatt points per 1,000 Bilt points[10]. Transfers at the current 1:1 remain possible through December 31, 2026. This is the second major transferable-points program to devalue Hyatt in a single month — Chase moved Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred cardholders (those who opened before June 15, 2026) from 1:1 to 4:3 on October 1[6], grandfathering nothing. Amex is not a Hyatt partner; Citi is not; Capital One is not. The 1:1 era for Hyatt is ending.

Chase → Hyatt 4:3 ratio now live for pre-June-2026 Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred[6]

Took effect October 1, 2026. 1,000 UR → 750 Hyatt for CSP and Ink Business Preferred cardholders who opened before June 15, 2026[6]. Sapphire Reserve, Chase Business Preferred, and newer-issued CSP/Ink BP accounts retain the 1:1 ratio. Pair this with Hyatt's May 2026 chart overhaul (three tiers to five, ceiling raised from 45,000 to 75,000 points per night)[4] and the sweet spots that defined this program for a decade — Alila Ventana Big Sur, Park Hyatts in Asia at Category 7 — are now stacked with both a devaluation and a transfer-rate hit.

IHG One Rewards: 3× points for voco stays, October 6 to December 6, 2026[11]

Earning promo, not a transfer bonus — IHG elite nights still accrue normally, but base points triple on voco brand stays through December 6[11]. voco is IHG's upper-upscale conversion brand, well-represented in Europe and Asia. For readers with an IHG stay already in the window, this is a routine 2× uplift on an otherwise forgettable category.
