Today's Transfer Bonuses

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards — 70%

Expires August 31, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 UR → 1,700 IHG

Four days left on Chase's tallest hotel-transfer bonus of the year. Chase stepped this promo down from a 100% early-bird window (Jul 15–30) to the current 70% band (Jul 31–Aug 31), which is a cool-off signal, not a warm-up — do not assume an extension.[1] IHG is only interesting for specific properties (Intercontinental Bora Bora Thalasso, Kimpton Seafire Grand Cayman, the higher-end Vignettes). At blended value roughly 0.5¢ per IHG point, this promo lifts each UR point to about 0.85¢ toward those properties — better than Chase Travel's baseline, but still below what the same UR points do at Hyatt at 1:1.

Your move: transfer only if you have specific IHG award nights held or a confirmed redemption target above 60,000 points/night; otherwise skip — a matched Chase→Hyatt redemption for the same trip beats this on almost every property.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Air Canada Aeroplan — 20% (30% for cardholders)

Runs August 12 – September 30, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 UR → 1,200 Aeroplan (1,300 with cardholder stack on transfers ≥50,000)

Chase's second airline bonus in a slow year for Chase airline promos. Chase Aeroplan cardholders can stack the standard 10% cardmember bonus (on single transfers of 50,000+ UR) with the 20% promo for an effective 30%, up to 25,000 bonus Aeroplan points per calendar year.[2] Aeroplan's Star Alliance chart still prices US–Europe business at 60,000 miles one-way in the lower distance band and US–Japan business at 55,000, making the effective cost of that seat with the stacked bonus roughly 42,000–46,000 UR one-way.

Your move: transfer only against a Star Alliance award you can hold now (Aeroplan holds free for 5 days for non-elites). Cardholders — pay attention to the 25,000-point annual stack cap; if you have already used it, the effective bonus is 20%, not 30%.

Amex Membership Rewards → British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus Avios — 30%

Runs August 3 – September 27, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 MR → 1,300 Avios (each of the three programs)

The workhorse Q3 Amex bonus, and the strongest single reason to have a live Membership Rewards balance right now. Avios shares fluidly across British Airways, Iberia, and Aer Lingus, so the useful redemptions are the same regardless of which program you route through: single-partner short-haul American Airlines flights from 7,500 Avios one-way, Iberia direct-flight Madrid business from 34,000, Aer Lingus Dublin business from 27,500 off-peak.[3][4] Surcharges are the caveat — BA and Iberia impose them on their own metal; American short-haul and Aer Lingus are the surcharge-clean routes.

Your move: if you fly American short-haul or use Aer Lingus for Ireland, transfer to whichever household program (usually BA or Aer Lingus AerClub) — pool is fungible after transfer. Do NOT transfer speculatively; the 30% bonus and 3-month cadence make it likely another one lands before EOY, though this is exactly the call in today's ledger.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Airlines Miles & Smiles — 40%

Expires August 31, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 TYP → 1,400 Miles & Smiles · Bonus capped at 50,000 bonus miles per member

Four days left on the strongest Citi bonus of the year and still one of the best transfer values available anywhere. Turkish's partner region-to-region chart holds business class North America–Europe at 65,000 miles one-way; with the 40% bonus that costs roughly 46,000 Citi points — cheaper than any Star Alliance program can price the same seat.[5] The catch is Turkish's booking channel: partner space is intermittent, calls to the Turkish call center are often required, and the miles land after the promo ends. The 50,000-bonus-mile cap means transfers above ~125,000 Citi points only earn the base rate.

Your move: transfer ONLY if you have located concrete Star Alliance award space (United, Lufthansa, Swiss, Air Canada) and confirmed a Turkish agent can see it. Do not transfer to speculate — Turkish miles are useful mainly for one narrow redemption pattern and are otherwise stranded.

Capital One miles → Avianca LifeMiles — 15%

Expires August 31, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 miles → 1,150 LifeMiles · No cap, no registration

The lowest-friction current bonus. No cap, no registration, no cardholder requirements — just the standard 1,000-mile minimum.[7] LifeMiles is the surcharge-free path onto Star Alliance business class, and the distance-based chart puts short-haul US business at 25,000 miles one-way and US–Europe business at 63,000. At 15% the effective cost of that trans-Atlantic seat is roughly 55,000 Capital One miles. The 15% is a step down from the 25% Capital One ran earlier in 2026, which is the reason it warrants only a cautious action.

Your move: transfer only against space held via LifeMiles' web tool; the risk on LifeMiles is IT — awards drop out of carts and phantom availability is common. If you can hold Star space with United first (24-hour free hold), do that before transferring.

Cathay Pacific Asia Miles (multi-bank, 6 programs) — 10%

Expires August 28, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 → 1,100 Asia Miles · Registration required · Capped at 10,000 bonus miles per member

The urgent-banner bonus — one day left. Eligible from Amex MR, Citi ThankYou, Capital One, Bilt, Wells Fargo, and Brex. The 10,000-bonus-mile cap means only the first 100,000 points transferred earn the bonus.[6] Cathay's Q4 all-partner bonuses in each of the last two years were bigger (30% in 2024, 20% in 2025) and uncapped, so today's terms look like Cathay setting a new floor rather than an anomaly.

Your move: transfer only if you have a specific Cathay or oneworld redemption held — Cathay business Hong Kong–US at 85,000 Asia Miles, JAL business North America–Tokyo at 60,000. Register before transferring or you get no bonus. If nothing is booked, skip: today's ledger call predicts the next multi-bank Cathay bonus will match or tighten these terms.

Program Watch

Hyatt: five-tier award chart is live (effective May 20, 2026)

Hyatt's expansion from three tiers (off-peak/standard/peak) to five (Lowest, Low, Moderate, Upper, Top) is now in force, along with 112 properties moving up one category. Peak pricing at flagship properties climbed as much as 67%; some off-season stays got mildly cheaper. Hyatt still values at 1.5–1.9¢ per point across the portfolio, so Chase UR at 1:1 remains the most valuable single transfer route in the transferable stack — but the trip-planning window widened dramatically.[8]

Your move: when comparing UR redemptions, price the exact night in the Hyatt web tool before transferring — the old rule of thumb ("1:1 always beats Chase Travel") no longer holds automatically at Upper/Top tier peak.

Marriott Bonvoy: quiet 2026 devaluation, 3–8% average, up to 15.6% at luxury

Marriott raised award prices across the portfolio in 2026 without publishing a chart change — the increases are 2.4–15.6%, clustered at 3–8%. Luxury and top-tier resorts took the heaviest hits.[9] Marriott is still valued at 0.7–0.85¢ per point, which pushes Amex MR → Bonvoy at 1:1 to a mediocre 0.75¢ per MR point.

Your move: no new transfer action; do not transfer MR → Bonvoy at 1:1 speculatively. The one exception is a confirmed 5th-night-free stay under the old point band, where the price rise may still net positive against cash.

United MileagePlus: cardholder award discount and PlusPoint changes now live

United's 2026 changes, phased through Feb 1, are now fully in force: cardholders get at least 10% off every award flight; PlusPoints and Complimentary Premier Upgrades apply to both revenue and award tickets; PQP thresholds for status settle at 6,000/12,000/18,000/28,000.[10] For a Chase UR holder without a United card, this is a small headwind — cardholders now have a durable pricing edge on the same seat.

Your move: if you regularly transfer UR to United, hold a United co-brand card (even the no-annual-fee TravelBank) to unlock the 10% discount before your next transfer — that alone can outweigh the annual fee.
