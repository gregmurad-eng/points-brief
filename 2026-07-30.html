A standing reminder that does not change: transfers are one-way and irreversible. Points that leave Chase, Amex, Citi or Capital One never come back — there is no undo, no support ticket, and no refund. Move points only with a specific redemption in mind, ideally one you have already confirmed is bookable. A transfer bonus is a discount on a purchase you were going to make anyway; it is never a reason to make the purchase.
