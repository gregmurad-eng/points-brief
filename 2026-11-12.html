Today’s Transfer Bonuses

Ten bonuses are active across Amex, Chase, Capital One and Citi today. Below is every offer that touches Greg’s stack (Chase, Amex, Hyatt, Marriott, IHG) plus the two Citi offers worth being aware of. Every percentage, expiry and transfer ratio was cross-checked against the three major trade desks[2][3][4].

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards — 80% bonus

Ratio 1,000 UR → 1,800 IHG · Expires December 3, 2026[4][5]

The headline number of the month, and also the trap. IHG points are commonly priced at or below 0.5 cents in value — and IHG sells points directly for roughly the same rate. Even at 1:1.8, you’re unlocking less than a cent per Chase point, well below the ~2 cent floor that makes a Chase transfer rational. Frequent Miler’s analyst is explicit: “that’s awful value, so it shouldn’t be done.”[5]

Your move: Skip unless you already have a specific IHG redemption booked or held, and have run the cash-vs-points math on that specific night. The 80% is bait — don’t speculate.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus Avios — 30% bonus

Ratio 1,000 UR → 1,300 Avios · Expires December 1, 2026[2][3]

The reliably useful one. Avios short-haul redemptions on JAL inside Japan, American Airlines short-haul domestic, and intra-Europe on BA/Iberia remain the Avios sweet spots that survived 2026’s wave of changes. At 1:1.3 a 9,000-Avios one-way on a US short-haul flight costs ~6,900 Chase points.

Your move: Transfer only if you have an award seat visible on ba.com or aa.com right now. Avios inventory is volatile and transfers are instant — search first, then transfer the exact amount needed.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Wyndham Rewards — 30% bonus

Ratio 1,000 UR → 1,300 Wyndham · Expires December 3, 2026[2]

Wyndham’s utility runs through the Vacasa vacation-rental partnership and Caesars conversion — niche uses most Chase holders won’t touch. The bonus doesn’t change the underlying math.

Your move: No action for most readers. Only relevant if you’re already planning a specific Vacasa or Caesars award and have run the numbers.

Amex Membership Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy — 35% bonus

Ratio 1,000 MR → 1,350 Bonvoy · Expires December 10, 2026[3][6]

Marriott’s pricing creep in 2026 — 5-10% average increases, Category 8-plus properties now pricing to 120,000 points at peak[7] — makes most speculative MR → Bonvoy transfers a bad idea. The 35% bonus narrows the gap but doesn’t close it: Amex MR is worth roughly twice as much as Bonvoy on a cents-per-point basis.

Your move: Transfer only if you’re booking a specific high-cash-rate stay where the math works at the stayed night. Don’t stockpile Bonvoy.

Amex Membership Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club — 30% bonus

Ratio 1,000 MR → 1,300 Virgin · Expires November 30, 2026[2][3]

The one actually-good transfer this month if you have the use case. Virgin’s sweet spots — ANA First and Business across the Pacific, Delta partner redemptions, and Air France La Première — survived 2026’s devaluations better than most. At 1.3x, MR holders can hit ANA First Class LAX→Tokyo for roughly 85K MR one-way (vs. 110K at base).

Your move: If you can locate ANA F or J award space on the dates you want (check the ANA site directly), this is the transfer worth making this month. Hold the seat before transferring — ANA space is the limiter, not the miles.

Capital One Miles → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club — 30% bonus

Ratio 1,000 CapOne → 1,300 Virgin · Expires November 20, 2026[2]

Same Virgin sweet spots as the Amex route, different pipe. Closes a week earlier than the Amex version.

Your move: If you hold Capital One miles and already identified the ANA or Delta partner redemption, go. Otherwise wait — Virgin Atlantic bonuses recur.

Capital One Miles → Air France-KLM Flying Blue — 20% bonus

Ratio 1,000 CapOne → 1,200 FB miles · Expires November 30, 2026[2]

Flying Blue’s own devaluation drift through 2026 means 20% barely compensates. Monthly Promo Rewards are still the Flying Blue sweet spot — but those cost miles you probably shouldn’t be speculatively transferring.

Your move: Skip unless you’ve locked a Promo Reward seat and need to top up by a small amount.

Capital One Miles → Copa ConnectMiles — 30% bonus

Ratio 1,000 CapOne → 975 ConnectMiles · Expires December 1, 2026[2][8]

Copa became Capital One’s 23rd transfer partner on November 2[8]. Note the base ratio: 1,000:750 (2:1.5). Even with the 30% launch bonus you end up with 975 ConnectMiles per 1,000 CapOne miles — still underwater versus the 1:1 baseline every other CapOne partner offers. Citi’s ThankYou route to Copa remains 1:1 for comparison.

Your move: Skip. This is a new partner, not a new sweet spot. Wait for a specific Copa saver award you want to book, then revisit.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles — 40% bonus (capped)

Ratio 1,000 TYP → 1,400 TK miles · Max 50,000 bonus miles · Expires November 30, 2026 23:59 Turkey time[9]

The best value in the stack this month if you hold Citi points. Turkish is Star Alliance, so the miles book United, Lufthansa, Air Canada, ANA economy and business at relatively fixed Turkish chart prices. The 50K bonus cap is hit by transferring 125K ThankYou points (yielding 175K Turkish miles). Bonus miles land after December 1[9].

Your move: Only if you hold Citi ThankYou points and the Turkish Airlines online system can find the award space you want (Turkish’s website is notoriously unreliable). Don’t open a Citi card for this.

Citi ThankYou → Cathay Pacific Asia Miles — 10% bonus

Ratio 1,000 TYP → 1,100 Asia Miles · Expires November 14, 2026[1]

Already surfaced in the urgent banner. 10% is below the threshold at which Asia Miles transfers become compelling, and two days isn’t enough runway to find and hold award space.

Your move: No action. Note the deadline, ignore the bonus.

Transfers are one-way and irreversible. Never transfer speculatively — confirm award availability on the airline or hotel site before moving a single point.

Program Watch

Bilt Rewards → World of Hyatt drops to 4:3 on January 1, 2027

Effective date: 2027-01-01 · Last day at 1:1: 2026-12-31[10][11]

Bilt’s World of Hyatt partner page now reads: “The transfer ratio for World of Hyatt will change to 4:3 beginning January 1, 2027.”[10] The 25% haircut applies regardless of Bilt elite status or card held. This follows Chase’s identical move for Sapphire Preferred and Ink Preferred (effective October 1, 2026). Chase Sapphire Reserve cards retain 1:1 for now.

Capital One adds Copa ConnectMiles as 23rd transfer partner

Effective November 2, 2026[8]

A real structural addition but with a poor base ratio: 2 Capital One miles yield 1.5 Copa miles (1,000:750). The 30% launch bonus still leaves transfers underwater. Capital One now has 23 partners; Copa is the thinnest of them on math.

Amex Membership Rewards adds Fanatics FanCash

Confirmed November 2026[12]

Eligible US cardmembers can now convert MR to FanCash at 1,000 MR = $10 FanCash — a 1 cent-per-point redemption rate. Mentioned here only to be explicit: this is a poor redemption and should not be treated as a sweet spot. Travel transfers still carry the only real MR value.

Today’s Play

Signal

If you hold Amex MR and want international premium cabin: locate ANA First or Business Class award space for your dates on the ANA site, then transfer Amex MR to Virgin Atlantic at the 30% bonus (1,000 → 1,300, through November 30). ANA F space is the binding constraint — miles are cheap, seats are not. Transfer the exact amount needed and book immediately; do not stockpile Virgin miles against vague future travel.

If you hold Chase UR and travel short-haul: the BA/Iberia/Aer Lingus 30% through December 1 is the second-best play, specifically for intra-Japan JAL or AA short-haul domestic. Same discipline: confirm the seat before transferring.

Pass on everything else. The 80% IHG, 35% Marriott, 20% Flying Blue, 30% Copa and 10% Cathay bonuses all fail the cents-per-point test for speculative transfers.
