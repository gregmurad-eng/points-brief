Today's Transfer Bonuses

Percentages below were corroborated across multiple established trade sources on the morning of publication. Issuer offer pages (Chase / Amex / Citi / Capital One transfer pages) require a cardholder login and were not independently retrieved for this edition — always verify the exact rate on your own account's transfer page before moving a single point. A transfer is one-way.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club — 40%

Runs through December 30, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 UR : 1,400 VA · Minimum 1,000 UR[3]

The strongest live outbound US transfer bonus right now, and it runs further into the year than any other current promo. Virgin Atlantic Flying Club is the standard tool for Delta One ex-US seats, ANA F/J from Europe, and the occasional Air France/KLM sweet spot. The usefulness is entirely downstream of whether you have award space held — points sitting in Flying Blue, VA, or Aeroplan aren't worth more than the award you can actually book with them.

Your move: transfer ONLY if you have confirmed partner award space on hold through Virgin Atlantic — otherwise park the points and let the 40% ride; this bonus has over five weeks left.

Amex Membership Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy — 35%

Runs through December 10, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 MR : 1,350 Marriott · Minimum 1,000 MR[2][1]

The MR → Marriott ratio is the industry's classic bad trade at baseline (1:1 for a hotel currency valued around 0.7¢). The 35% drags it to 1:1.35, still a redemption-value call rather than an outright win — you're paying roughly 1.4 MR points per Marriott point, so you need a Marriott redemption that beats what the MR could do elsewhere. With Marriott moving fully to dynamic pricing in 2026 and card changes landing in February 2027 (see Program Watch), MR-funded Marriott speculation is riskier than it looks.

Your move: transfer ONLY against a specific Marriott reservation whose cash rate is at least 0.9¢ per Marriott point — do not stockpile Bonvoy on this bonus.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards — 80%

Runs through December 3, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 UR : 1,800 IHG · Minimum 1,000 UR[4]

The biggest headline percentage of the month, and the most misleading one. IHG points carry a thin cash-equivalent value; even at 1:1.8 the implied UR cost per IHG point sits below one cent, which is roughly what IHG's own buy-points promos currently charge. Frequent Miler's position — "don't" — is worth taking seriously. The bonus is only useful if you've already priced a specific IHG redemption and found UR to be the cheapest funding source for it.

Your move: pass by default; transfer ONLY if you've priced the exact IHG award in UR, Points & Cash, and IHG's current buy-points rate and UR comes out cheapest.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles — 40%

Runs through November 30, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 TYP : 1,400 Miles&Smiles · 50,000 bonus-miles cap[5]

Turkish is the Star Alliance sweet-spot program readers come to Citi ThankYou for in the first place — 7,500 miles one-way intra-US on United (any distance), 45,000 miles for United business class to Europe when space loads. The 50k cap on bonus miles means you can push at most 125,000 TYP through this promo to realize the full bonus. The chronic friction remains Turkish itself: phone-booking, inconsistent website, and the occasional multi-week wait. None of that is a reason to stockpile.

Your move: transfer ONLY if you have a specific Turkish-bookable award located (United/Air Canada/ANA/Lufthansa saver space) and have a backup partner ready in case Turkish's agents refuse to see it.

Amex Membership Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club — 30%

Runs through November 30, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 MR : 1,300 VA · Minimum 1,000 MR[1]

Direct competitor to the Chase 40% — same destination program, lower rate, earlier expiry. The only reason to use this instead of Chase → Virgin Atlantic is if you hold MR but not UR, or if a specific award requires you to keep UR in reserve for a different partner (Hyatt, Southwest, Aer Lingus). Otherwise this bonus is strictly dominated.

Your move: skip in favor of Chase → VA at 40% unless you don't hold UR, or your UR is already earmarked for a different transfer.

Capital One Miles → Air France/KLM Flying Blue — 20%

Runs through November 30, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 C1 : 1,200 Flying Blue · Minimum 1,000 C1[1]

Flying Blue's monthly Promo Rewards (reduced-mileage awards on specific SkyTeam routes) are the only place a 20% bonus like this reliably earns its keep — chasing the standard chart with Flying Blue rarely does. Check Flying Blue's current Promo Rewards list before transferring, not after.

Your move: transfer ONLY against an open Promo Reward you can book immediately, not on speculation that one will appear.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Avios (British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus) — 30%

Runs through December 1, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 UR : 1,300 Avios · Minimum 1,000 UR[1]

Avios is more useful for short-haul domestic-equivalent hops (Aer Lingus trans-Atlantic non-peak, intra-Europe Iberia, Qatar Qsuite booked via BA Avios) than for long-haul BA in premium. 2026's Avios revaluations have raised the cost of nearly every top-end redemption; the 30% bonus does not reverse those changes, it softens them.

Your move: transfer ONLY against a specific Avios redemption priced out in advance; do not buy Avios to "have them" at 30%.

Program Watch

World of Hyatt announces sweeping 2027 changes (effective January 1, 2027)

Source: Hyatt announcement, November 18, 2026[8][9]

Hyatt is restructuring Milestone Rewards so that benefits begin at 10 qualifying nights rather than 20, and is introducing stackable awards (Cat 1-4, Cat 1-7, Ultimate and promotional Free Night Awards can all combine with a Suite Upgrade Award on one stay) alongside a standardized 365-day validity on all awards. The visible cost: Globalist free parking is sunsetting — a new separate Free Parking Award replaces it on both paid and award stays for up to seven nights, and Globalists at year-end 2026 keep the legacy free-parking benefit on award nights only through their next tier expiry or February 29, 2028, whichever comes first.

Your move: do not transfer to Hyatt speculatively on this news — the structural levers (Milestone Rewards, stacking) favor stays you're already planning; the Globalist-parking change is a value cut, not a reason to buy more points.

Marriott Bonvoy Amex cards: Free Night Award now gated by $3,000 spend (effective February 9, 2027)

Sources: Marriott / Amex notices, November 2026[10]

Starting February 9, 2027, the annual Free Night Award on the Marriott Bonvoy Amex and Marriott Bonvoy Business Amex will require $3,000 in annual card spend before it issues — previously it was automatic on renewal. Amex is also introducing a no-annual-fee Marriott Bonvoy Bright. This is structural, not a promo, and it reframes the "keep for the free night" math on both cards.

Your move: if you hold the annual-fee Marriott Amex and don't naturally hit $3,000 of card spend per year, re-run the keep/downgrade decision before February 9, 2027 — the free night was the whole retention argument on the personal card.

Capital One → Copa ConnectMiles 30% bonus still leaves you below 1:1

Sources: Capital One transfer-partner page; Frequent Miler[7]

Capital One added Copa Airlines ConnectMiles as a transfer partner on November 2, 2026 with a launch 30% transfer bonus through December 1, 2026 — but the base Capital One → Copa ratio is 2:1.5, not 1:1. Even with the 30% bonus, 1,000 Capital One miles yields roughly 975 Copa miles. Citi ThankYou and Rove transfer to Copa at 1:1 without needing any bonus to clear parity. A headline number that looks like value can still be a losing trade when the base ratio is below one.

Your move: do not transfer Capital One to Copa even with this bonus — use Citi ThankYou or Rove if you need Copa miles for a Star Alliance sweet-spot redemption.

Amex Hilton 40% is a UK-only offer

Source: cross-market check of US and UK Amex pages[11]

Several roundups are circulating a "40% Amex → Hilton transfer bonus through November 24, 2026." It is a UK Amex promotion (Amex UK → Hilton Honors). No comparable bonus is currently live on US Amex Membership Rewards. Cross-border offer confusion is a common source of accidental transfers; worth a flag on a day when the UK offer is in its final 48 hours.

Your move: do not attempt to transfer US Amex Membership Rewards to Hilton Honors expecting a 40% boost — that bonus does not apply to US cardholders.

Today's Play

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club at 40% is the one bonus worth acting on today — and only against a held award.

Of everything live right now, the Chase → Virgin Atlantic promo is the strongest combination of headline rate (40%), runway (through December 30), and a program that still produces reliably bookable high-value redemptions (Delta One ex-US, ANA F/J ex-Europe during peak release windows). But the discipline is unchanged: a transfer is one-way. The 40% is not a reason to transfer; the held award is the reason to transfer, and the 40% is why you fund the award from UR rather than another currency.

Everything else live today either fails a redemption test (IHG at 80%, Capital One → Copa at any rate), has a dominant alternative (Amex → VA 30% is strictly worse than Chase → VA 40%), or needs a specific existing plan to be worth triggering (Marriott 35%, Flying Blue 20%, Turkish 40%, Avios 30%). If you don't have an award on hold, today's correct action is: do nothing.
