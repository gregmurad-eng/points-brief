Today’s Transfer Bonuses

Nine transfer promos are live heading into the second week of October. Four of them matter if your stack is Chase and Amex. The Chase to Marriott 70% and the Amex to Hilton 30% both expire in the next ten to eleven days, so they are the clock-watchers. Everything else runs through month-end.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy — 70%[1]

A record-high 70% bonus runs from September 15 through October 15, 2026 at 11:59 pm ET. Transfer ratio is 1,000 Chase points to 1,700 Marriott Bonvoy points, 1,000-point minimum. Base points transfer instantly; the bonus portion can take up to seven days to post[2]. The headline percentage is also the problem: Marriott Bonvoy points are worth roughly 0.73 cents each per TPG’s October valuations, so even after the 70% bump you’re redeeming Chase points at about 1.24 cents each — well below Chase’s 2.05-cent portal baseline[9].

transfer only if you have a specific Marriott redemption booked where the all-in cash rate is above $300 per night and award availability is confirmed. Topping off a stay you’ve already locked is the honest use case; speculative transfers to a dynamically-priced hotel chart are not.

Amex Membership Rewards → Hilton Honors — 30% (targeted)[3]

Amex is offering a 30% bonus to Hilton Honors through October 14, 2026 at a 1:2.6 transfer ratio — 1,000 MR becomes 2,600 Hilton Honors points. AwardWallet notes this appears to be a targeted offer; log in to your Amex account to see if the promo tile is live for you.

check your Amex account first — if the tile is there, Hilton is one of the few places transferring MR works out, especially for aspirational Hilton properties with $600+ cash rates. If the tile isn’t there, don’t force it; the standard 1:2 ratio stays the single worst use of MR points at any time.

Amex Membership Rewards → Flying Blue — 25%[4]

Open to all MR cardholders, no targeting. Runs October 1 through October 31, 2026 at 11:59 pm MT, 1:1.25 transfer ratio (1,000 MR = 1,250 Flying Blue miles). Unlimited redemptions, 999,999-point per-transaction cap, no stated minimum[5]. This is the most useful bonus on this list for most readers: Flying Blue transatlantic economy awards start around 20,000 miles one-way at Promo Awards pricing, which this bonus takes down to 16,000 MR per leg.

transfer only to book — Flying Blue Promo Rewards drop and refill constantly, so queue a specific date and route first, confirm partner space on AF/KLM/Delta/Virgin, then move exactly what you need. Do not transfer a round balance speculatively.

Capital One Miles → British Airways Avios — 20%[6]

Through October 31, 2026, Capital One is offering a 20% bonus on transfers to British Airways Executive Club. Ratio is 1:1.2 (1,000 miles = 1,200 Avios). Last Capital One bonus before this one was 30% to JAL Mileage Bank in September.

pass unless you’re targeting short-haul American or Alaska partner awards — BA Avios shine for 4,750-mile hops on American inside the US and for Iberia transatlantic business during off-peak. BA metal itself, especially between North America and London, remains a surcharge trap.

Citi ThankYou Rewards → Qatar Privilege Club Avios — up to 35%[7]

Citi is running a tiered bonus to Qatar Privilege Club Avios through October 31, 2026, scaling up to 35% at the higher transfer tiers[10]. Qatar Avios can be used across the oneworld network and are the best-value route to Qsuites award space between the US and the Middle East or Asia.

worth it only if you can hold Qsuites space on QR or JAL business before transferring — Qatar Avios inventory is dynamic and the 35% bonus is wasted if you’re speculating. Build the itinerary in the Qatar portal first, then push the exact point total across.

Program Watch

Chase → World of Hyatt dropped to 4:3 for Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred

The devaluation Chase announced in June went live three days ago, on October 1, 2026. Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred cardholders now get 750 Hyatt points per 1,000 Chase points — a 25% effective haircut. Sapphire Reserve and Reserve Business cardholders keep the 1:1 ratio[11][12]. This is a structural change to one of the three or four most valuable transfer pairings in the US market and reshapes which Chase card you should be putting hotel spend on.

Delta and Hyatt announced a new dual-earning partnership

Delta SkyMiles and World of Hyatt revealed a loyalty tie-up allowing dual-earn on paid Hyatt stays and status crossover benefits[13]. Full enrollment mechanics and effective dates haven’t been finalized in public, so treat this as a 2027 story for now, not an immediate action.

Wyndham Rewards introduced a fourth award tier at 45,000 points

Wyndham moved from a three-tier to a four-tier award chart. The former 7,500-point floor was reduced to 5,000, but roughly 2,040 mid-range properties jumped from 15,000 to 30,000 points per night. The new top band tops out at 45,000[14]. Net effect for most redeemers is a mid-chart devaluation disguised by a lower entry point.

Today’s Play

The Chase to Marriott 70% is the headline promo this week, but the Chase to Hyatt 4:3 change that went live Wednesday is the move that actually matters. If you have a Sapphire Preferred or Ink Business Preferred and were sitting on Chase points earmarked for a Hyatt redemption, your effective rate just worsened 25%. The playbook splits two ways:

If you hold Sapphire Reserve: ignore the Marriott 70% unless you have a specific Bonvoy stay booked. Your 1:1 Hyatt pipe is now the single most valuable transfer route in the Chase ecosystem and should be hoarded for it.

If you hold only Sapphire Preferred or Ink Business Preferred: you lost 25% on the only Chase transfer partner that reliably beat the portal. The Marriott 70% still math-fails at ~1.24 cpp vs the 2.05-cent portal baseline, so don’t chase it to compensate. The better move this month is Amex to Flying Blue 25% if you have MR, since that pairing still clears the baseline math on a confirmed award.

confirm which Chase card you have, then decide. If anyone opened a Sapphire Reserve this year specifically for the Hyatt protection, this is the week it starts paying for itself.
Reminder: every transfer on this page is one-way and irreversible. Confirm award availability inside the destination program’s own search before transferring. A “good deal” with no booked award at the other end is just a stranded point balance in a worse currency.
