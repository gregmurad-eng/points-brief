Lead Story

Virgin Atlantic hits 30% from both sides — Amex and Chase at once

It is rare for the same sweet-spot partner to light up on two currencies simultaneously, but that is exactly where July stands. American Express is running a 30% bonus to Virgin Atlantic Flying Club through July 31, and Chase Ultimate Rewards is matching the rate at 30% through July 14. For anyone sitting on both stacks, Flying Club is momentarily the most productive place your points can land.

Why it matters: Flying Club remains one of the best-kept partner engines in the game — think ANA first and business class, Delta One on transatlantic metal, and Virgin's own reasonable saver awards. At a 30% bonus, 100k transferred becomes 130k miles, which can be the difference between a saver seat and a stretch. The usual caution applies: transfer only against confirmed availability, because the move is one-way. If you are not ready to book, let the points sit — the bonus rate is nice, but stranded miles in Flying Club help no one.

Today's Briefs

Hyatt's summer promo rewards the second stay

World of Hyatt's global summer offer gives 2,000 bonus points for every two nights, up to 8,000, for stays July 1 through September 7. The catch worth knowing: only your second qualifying stay starts counting toward the offer, so a single long trip earns less than two shorter ones. Register before you check in.

Marriott stacks a summer base offer with luxury bonuses

Bonvoy is running 3,000 bonus points per stay in the U.S. and Canada (code S2449, through Sept. 8), plus a richer 10,000-points-per-stay offer at its luxury brands — Ritz-Carlton, St. Regis, W, JW and the Luxury Collection — on two-night-plus stays booked by August 31. If a luxury weekend was already on the calendar, register both.

The Big Three quietly converge on the same math

Delta and American now sit on an identical 5-to-11-miles-per-dollar earning ladder, and United's latest MileagePlus tweaks push the same revenue-first logic even harder toward co-branded cardholders. The practical takeaway for 2026: where you earn matters less than which card you carry, and elite-chasing on spend alone keeps getting more expensive.

United's status challenge window stays open

United's published MileagePlus status challenge is accepting requests through December 21, 2026 — useful leverage if you hold status elsewhere and fly enough United to fast-track a match rather than requalify from zero.
