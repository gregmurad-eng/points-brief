Chase UR → World of Hyatt cuts to 4:3 for Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred on October 1

The single most consequential structural change of Q4 lands in 3 days. Existing Chase Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred cardholders have transferred to World of Hyatt at 1:1 since October 2018 — as of Thursday, October 1, 2026, that ratio becomes 4:3 (1,000 UR → 750 Hyatt points), a roughly 25% haircut on what most trade sites had called the single strongest transfer relationship in the US card ecosystem. Only Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Reserve for Business, and J.P. Morgan Reserve retain 1:1. This is the first time Chase has ever bonused-or-cut on the Hyatt route.[10][11]

Do not panic-transfer to Hyatt just to beat the deadline. Points parked in a Hyatt balance are exposed to Hyatt's May 2026 award-chart devaluation risk continuing, and you cannot undo a transfer. If you have a Cat 6 or 7 aspirational award booked (or bookable) in the next 12 months and don't hold Sapphire Reserve, this deadline is real for you. If your Hyatt use is Cat 1–4 short domestic stays, one 25% haircut on future transfers is materially cheaper than moving your entire balance to a currency that itself is devaluing.
