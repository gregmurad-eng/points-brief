Chase Ultimate Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy — 70% [3][4][5][6]

Ratio 1,000 : 1,700 (bonus stacks on the base 1:1). Live Sep 15 – Oct 15, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET. Bonus points post to Bonvoy within 7 days of the transfer.

Today's Play

The trap this month is treating "highest-ever" as "must transfer." The Chase→Marriott 70% bonus is a genuine, well-corroborated headline, and the industry is loud about it. But the underlying math — Marriott points at roughly 0.7 cents, plus persistent flash devaluations — means the 70% only becomes a good decision when it is stacked onto a specific award already priced out. A 70% bonus applied to a bad redemption is still a bad redemption.

The right frame this week: the Citi→LHW 25% expiring Friday is the most time-critical decision, but only for people with LHW plans; the Chase→Marriott 70% is the loudest but demands the strictest discipline; the Amex→Avios 30% is the most forgiving and has the deepest, most-repeatable use cases.
