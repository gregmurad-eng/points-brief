Today's Transfer Bonuses

Amex MR → Hilton Honors — 30%

Transfer ratio: 1 : 2.6 (base 1:2, with 30% boost). Window: ends Oct 14, 2026.[1][2]

The last Hilton MR bonus of 2026 at the ceiling percentage. Hilton redemption rates are dynamic and often expensive in points, so transfer only if you've already pulled up a specific booking and the math works at ~0.5¢/point.

Your move: transfer only if you have a specific Hilton stay on hold where the award cost confirmed before the bonus produces at least 0.6¢/MR point. Otherwise skip — Hilton usually bonuses MR once more in Q1.

Chase UR → Marriott Bonvoy — 70%

Transfer ratio: 1,000 UR → 1,700 Bonvoy. Window: ends Oct 15, 2026 11:59pm ET. Posting: up to 7 days.[3][4]

This matches Chase's historical ceiling for a UR→Marriott promotion and almost certainly won't be matched again before mid-2027. With Marriott's 2026 category changes raising 71 flagship properties and overall points worth ~0.7¢ each, this gets each transferred UR point to roughly 1.19¢ at Marriott — below the 1.75¢+ you'd get redeeming UR for Hyatt or quality airline awards, but potentially worth it if you have an aspirational Marriott redemption you've been sitting on.[5]

Your move: transfer only against a specific Marriott booking that's within 7 days of your intended stay date (the posting lag is real). Do not pre-fund a Bonvoy balance speculatively — dynamic pricing can erase the bonus the day after your transfer lands.

Citi ThankYou → JAL Mileage Bank — 30%

Transfer ratio: 1,000 TY → 1,300 JAL miles (premium cards 1:1 base; no-annual-fee cards 1:0.7 base, so bonus is on reduced base). Window: Sep 20 – Oct 24, 2026.[6]

Launch bonus for Citi's newest transfer partner. JAL Mileage Bank is one of the better uses of a flexible-currency transfer when it matches: oneworld partner awards (First class to Japan, intra-Asia on JAL/Qatar/Cathay) frequently price well below AA or BA. Partner awards must be booked by phone and JAL is strict about award ticket name matches.

Your move: transfer only if you've already called JAL and held partner award space on a specific routing. For speculative transfers, wait — a 30% launch bonus is not the last one you'll see, but booking phone-only partner awards without held inventory is how people end up with stranded JAL miles.

Citi ThankYou → Avianca LifeMiles — 25%

Transfer ratio: 1,000 TY → 1,250 LifeMiles (premium cards). Window: Sep 20 – Oct 24, 2026.[7]

Avianca is still one of the most generous Star Alliance redemption charts for intra-US (7.5K United-operated short-haul one-way) and Lufthansa First when it opens up to partners. Reliability has slipped over the last 18 months — expect to need to call Avianca when partner space doesn't ticket.

Your move: transfer only to top up for a specific booking you can see available in the Avianca search tool today. Avoid parking large LifeMiles balances — program-change risk is non-trivial even with the bonus.

Citi ThankYou → Qatar Privilege Club Avios — up to 35%

Tiered: 15% bonus on <50,000; 25% on 50,000-99,999; 35% on 100,000+. Transfer ratio 1:1 base. Window: Oct 1 – Oct 31, 2026. Bonus Avios post by Nov 30.[8][9]

The 35% tier triggers only at 100K+ in a single transfer, so this is a bonus structured for premium-cabin aspirational redemptions, not short-haul top-ups. Qatar Avios can book Qsuite with no fuel surcharges and are useful for Qatar-operated intra-Africa and intra-Asia pricing. Bonus is processed on Qatar's end, so your Citi transfer screen will show the un-bonused amount.

Your move: at 100K+ to confirmed Qsuite space you've already found in Qatar's search tool, this is one of the better Citi redemptions of Q4. Below 50K the tiered bonus structure means you're accepting 15% — hold out for a 25%+ flat Qatar bonus unless you need the miles now.

Amex MR → AeroMexico Rewards — 20%

Transfer ratio: 1,000 MR → 1,200 Rewards. Window: through Oct 30, 2026.[10]

AeroMexico Rewards is one of Amex's less-covered partners, and 20% bonuses on it are infrequent. Rewards is useful for Delta-operated flights at Delta-partner pricing without SkyMiles' peak surge, and for intra-Mexico and short LatAm segments.

Your move: skip unless you have a specific AeroMexico or Delta codeshare already price-checked at Rewards' partner chart. The partner isn't valuable enough on its own to transfer speculatively, even at 20%.

Amex MR → Air France-KLM Flying Blue — 25%

Transfer ratio: 1,000 MR → 1,250 Flying Blue. Window: through Oct 31, 2026. Min 1,000 / max 999,000 per transfer.[11]

Flying Blue monthly Promo Rewards remain one of the most reliable sweet spots in the Amex ecosystem — business class to Europe routinely drops to ~55-62K one-way at the current Flying Blue monthly promo cadence. The 25% is middling (we've seen 30-40% in 2026) but it stacks against a Promo Rewards flight that's already discounted.

Your move: transfer only against a Flying Blue Promo Rewards fare you've already found for your travel dates. For general top-ups, wait — Amex has run 30% Flying Blue bonuses twice this year already.

Capital One Miles → British Airways Club Avios — 20%

Transfer ratio: 1,000 C1 → 1,200 Avios. Window: Oct 1 – Oct 31, 2026.[12]

Capital One typically runs this bonus to BA once a year, so this is likely the last Capital One → BA bonus until late 2027. BA Avios are useful for short-haul AA domestic (Reward Flight Saver on AA metal), intra-Europe hops on BA/Iberia/Vueling, and the Qatar Qsuite transatlantic sweet spot via Avios combinability.

Your move: if BA Avios figure in a trip you're actively planning for Q4 or Q1, this is the one annual window. If not, skip — Capital One miles are more flexible held in C1 than parked in BA.

Program Watch

United MileagePlus status match offer closes October 15

United's limited-time status match for Delta Medallion and AAdvantage elite members closes October 15, 2026. Approved members get 90 days of United elite status and must then hit challenge spend thresholds to extend through January 31, 2028.[13] This is the first large-scale status match United has run in the current competitive cycle; it's targeted at the Chicago, Denver and SFO hub bleed to Delta's cross-hub routes.

Your move: file only if you can realistically hit the challenge spend inside 90 days on a route pattern you'll actually fly. A 90-day paper match that lapses in January is worse than no match.

American launches Cash + Miles without disclosing a mile value

American Airlines began rolling out mixed cash-and-miles bookings on aa.com and the AA app the week of October 2, 2026, starting with domestic AA-operated flights (excluding Alaska and Hawaii routes).[14] As Points Crowd flagged, AA notably declined to disclose the per-mile cash-equivalent value used in the mixed-payment flow.[15] Delta's comparable Pay with Miles has historically paid out around 1.0¢/mile — well below redemption value for award tickets.

Your move: treat Cash + Miles as a last-resort tool for small balance liquidation, not a redemption strategy. Until AA publishes the exchange rate, assume ≤1.0¢/mile and compare any Cash + Miles screen to the all-miles award price at that fare class.

AAdvantage Business 15,000-mile new-member bonus through Oct 31

Through October 31, 2026, newly-registered AAdvantage Business members earn 15,000 AAdvantage bonus miles using promo code GET15K.[16] Business-side AAdvantage mile posting is routed to the registered personal AAdvantage account, so the miles are usable like any other AAdvantage balance.

Your move: if you have any business entity — even sole-proprietor — you can enroll in AAdvantage Business free. 15,000 AAdvantage miles for a 5-minute enrollment is a one-way-trip award at Reduced Mileage levels. Register before October 31.
