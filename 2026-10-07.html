Chase Ultimate Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy · 70% [2][4]

Expires October 15, 2026 · Ratio 1,000 UR → 1,700 Marriott Bonvoy

Eight days left. This 70% bonus matches the all-time high Chase has offered on this transfer corridor, and the window from announcement to expiry has been one of the shortest Chase has run on Marriott in the last two years[2]. The pressure test, now that the Chase→Hyatt corridor has been clipped to 4:3 on CSP and Ink Business Preferred[11], is whether Marriott is worth parking UR in at all for anyone who was previously running the Chase→Hyatt playbook. Do not bulk-move UR to Marriott as a hedge against further Hyatt devaluation; Marriott's own award chart moved up 5–10% across the portfolio this year[13], and the same erosion risk applies to the destination.

United MileagePlus partner-award pricing continues to drift up [12]

Effective throughout 2026 · United-side rate adjustments without published notice

Trade coverage through September and early October points to continued intra-Europe and intra-Asia business-class rate increases on partner awards booked through United, with Tokyo partner business reported as high as 140,000 miles one-way[12]. United's historical pattern is to adjust partner pricing without announcement, which makes any specific redemption a snapshot in time rather than a chart commitment. Trade sources are increasingly recommending booking the same partner metal through Aeroplan or ANA Mileage Club where published charts still bind.

Before transferring Chase UR into United for a partner award, price the same itinerary via Aeroplan (also a Chase, Amex, and Capital One partner) and ANA Mileage Club (Amex partner). On many trans-Atlantic and trans-Pacific partner awards, Aeroplan is now cheaper than United on identical metal. Do not transfer UR into United speculatively against a hoped-for sweet spot — United's rate for the exact flight can and does change between the time you move the points and the time you try to book.

World of Hyatt dynamic-chart cycle has had five months to settle [13]

Chart change effective May 20, 2026 · Three categories collapsed to five; 112 properties moved up, 24 down

Not news this week, but newly relevant given the Chase→Hyatt devaluation above. Hyatt's May move from a three-tier fixed chart to a five-tier dynamic chart has now had five months of data. Mid-tier properties where the cash rate is moderate have broadly settled around the published midpoints; aspirational top-tier properties (Alila, Park Hyatt flagships) are routinely at or near Peak pricing on availability dates most people actually want to travel[13]. The era of "transfer UR to Hyatt and get flat 30k nights at Park Hyatts" is over for most dates.

If you still hold a 1:1 Chase→Hyatt corridor (Chase Sapphire Reserve), run Hyatt pricing BEFORE transferring, not after. Dynamic pricing means two guests on the same date can see different points totals for the same room; always check your own account at the exact date range before committing a transfer.
