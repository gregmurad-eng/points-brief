Today's Transfer Bonuses

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy — 70% (expires Oct 15, 2026)[1][2][3]

Chase's largest-ever UR→Marriott bonus. 1,000 UR transfers to 1,700 Bonvoy at the standard 1:1 base, bonus points land in the Marriott account within 7 days of transfer. No minimum stated beyond Chase's normal 1,000-point transfer increment.

transfer only if you already have a specific Marriott redemption held or a strong candidate priced. At 70% the effective per-UR rate is still only ~1.2¢ against Marriott's ~0.7¢-per-point street value, which means this bonus pays off ONLY for aspirational Cat 7-8 or long-weekend 5th-night-free stacks. Transferring to speculate wastes the record ratio.

Citi ThankYou → Japan Airlines Mileage Bank — 30% (expires Oct 24, 2026)[1][3][4]

Citi's launch bonus for the new JAL partnership (JAL became Citi's 24th airline partner on September 20). 1,000 TY transfers to 1,300 JAL miles. Base ratio 1:1.

wait unless you have a specific Oneworld or JAL metal redemption identified. JAL partner-award sweet spots exist (US to Hawaii in business for ~60k, intra-Asia for 25k) but availability is thin and JAL's own site is the authoritative search — do not transfer blind, because JAL miles are far less fungible than Avios or Flying Blue.

Rove Miles → Japan Airlines Mileage Bank — 50% (expires Oct 31, 2026)[1][5]

The highest JAL transfer bonus from any flexible-points program in 2026. 1,000 Rove transfers to 1,500 JAL. Base ratio 1:1.

if you earn Rove and have a confirmed JAL partner-award hold, this beats Citi's 30%. If you're choosing where to speculate between Rove's three Oct-31 bonuses — JAL at 50%, Flying Blue at 40%, Qatar at up to 35% — JAL is the highest headline rate but Flying Blue is the most liquid currency and still clears 1:1.4. No action if Rove isn't already part of your stack.

Rove Miles → Air France/KLM Flying Blue — 40% (expires Oct 31, 2026)[1][5][6]

1,000 Rove transfers to 1,400 Flying Blue miles. Base ratio 1:1. Overlaps with Amex MR's own 25% Flying Blue bonus — Rove is the higher-rate inflow for Flying Blue right now.

transfer only against a specific Flying Blue Promo Rewards fare or a confirmed partner-award hold (Virgin Atlantic to the US, Delta short-haul). Flying Blue's September 8 tiered-fare rollout raised AF/KLM Standard business from ~60k to ~75k one-way, so speculative hoarding is explicitly worse than it was a month ago.

Qatar Airways Privilege Club Avios — 15 to 35% from Citi ThankYou (expires Oct 31, 2026)[7][8][4]

Tiered bonus structure: 15% on transfers up to 49,999 Avios, 25% on 50,000–99,999, 35% on 100,000+ Avios. The 35% tier requires a single transaction of 100,000+ Avios, calculated per transaction not cumulatively. Citi is the only US bank program eligible for this promo (Rove is also eligible, at the same tier structure). Base ratio 1:1. Bonus Avios post by November 30.

transfer only if you have a specific Qatar Qsuite or Oneworld partner redemption identified and the 100,000+ Avios one-shot threshold aligns with your target cost. Smaller transfers at the 15% tier are weaker than Rove's flat 35% to Qatar for the same tier — if you have both currencies, favour Rove at the small-transfer end.

Amex Membership Rewards → Hilton Honors — 30% (expires Oct 14, 2026)[1][3]

1,000 MR transfers to 2,600 Hilton (the elevated transfer includes Amex's standard 1:2 base ratio plus the 30% bonus). 11 days remain.

this is Amex's fall Hilton push — standard Amex→Hilton bonuses have run at this level roughly once a year. Transfer only against a specific high-category Hilton (Waldorf, Conrad, Maldives) where the ~0.5¢-per-MR rate finally outruns Amex's airline options. If you don't have a Hilton booking in mind, let it pass.

Amex Membership Rewards → Air France/KLM Flying Blue — 25% (expires Oct 31, 2026)[1][9][6]

1,000 MR transfers to 1,250 Flying Blue miles at 1:1 base.

transfer only against a confirmed Promo Rewards fare or a Virgin Atlantic / Delta short-haul hold. If you're choosing between this and Rove's 40% to the same destination, Rove is the better inflow when both currencies are available. If MR is your only option, 25% is Amex's typical Flying Blue level — do not treat as a "while supplies last" moment.

Amex Membership Rewards → AeroMexico Rewards — 20% (expires Oct 30, 2026)[1][3]

1,000 MR transfers to 1,200 AeroMexico points. Base ratio 1:1.

no action unless you have a specific AeroMexico or Delta partner-award in the works. AeroMexico Rewards is a revenue-based program — speculative transfers rarely pay back the sticker rate.

Citi ThankYou → Avianca LifeMiles — 25% (expires Oct 24, 2026)[1][3]

1,000 TY transfers to 1,250 LifeMiles. Base ratio 1:1.

LifeMiles' distance-based Star Alliance chart (25k short-haul business, 63k US→Europe business) is one of the strongest redemptions reachable at this bonus rate — but miles don't expire and LifeMiles routinely runs 25%+ bonuses two or three times a year. Transfer against a held award or wait.

Capital One Miles → British Airways Club — 20% (expires Oct 31, 2026)[1][3]

1,000 Cap One miles transfer to 1,200 BA Avios at 1:1 base. This is also Capital One's only live bonus — call 2026-10-01-1 in the ledger specifically tested whether Cap One would run anything in October, and this is the answer.

worth it ONLY for Avios-specific sweet spots — AA short-haul domestic (7.5k off-peak), Qatar Qsuite redemptions, Iberia short-haul to Madrid. Avios are freely interchangeable across BA, Iberia, Aer Lingus, Qatar, and Finnair, so pooling before a specific booking is the usual play.

Program Watch

Chase Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred: 1:1 Hyatt route cut to 4:3 — landed Oct 1, 2026[10][11]

Announced June 15 as part of the Sapphire Preferred refresh. Effective October 1, Chase Sapphire Preferred and Chase Ink Business Preferred cardholders now transfer UR to World of Hyatt at 4:3 — 1,000 UR becomes 750 Hyatt points, a 25% haircut on what had been one of the two or three most valuable routes in the entire transferable-points stack. Chase Sapphire Reserve and Sapphire Reserve for Business keep 1:1. The segmentation is explicit: Hyatt transfer value is now a premium-tier benefit.

if you only hold Sapphire Preferred or Ink Preferred and have no plans to upgrade to Reserve, Hyatt is effectively a hotel-partner-only redemption now — move earned balances toward BA Avios short-haul or Virgin Atlantic sweet spots instead. If you have a Reserve or Reserve Business in the household, the household transfer-share trick (Chase lets you combine UR between household members on different cards) still works and may be worth setting up before further changes.

Bilt Rewards: 1:1 Hyatt route cut to 4:3 effective January 1, 2027[12][13]

Announced October 1, 2026 — same day as Chase's cutover. All Bilt members, every card tier (including the $495 Palladium), will transfer Bilt to Hyatt at 4:3 starting Jan 1. No carve-out for elite card tiers. December 31 is the published lock-in deadline for 1:1 transfers. Context: Bilt and Chase were the only two major flexible-points programs with any direct Hyatt route; Amex, Citi, and Capital One do not transfer to Hyatt at all.

if you have Bilt points you intended to park against future Hyatt stays, model a before-Dec-31 transfer against the specific property you'll redeem at — a 4:3 cut means you need 33% more Bilt points for the same stay post-cutover. But do NOT pre-transfer speculative balances: Hyatt category creep (see call 2026-09-29-1) and the May 2026 chart devaluation have already weakened aspirational redemptions, and parking points at Hyatt now also exposes you to further Hyatt-side devaluation. The ratio cut is real; the urgency to pre-transfer without a specific redemption in hand is not.

World of Hyatt Q4 2026 Bonus Journeys promotion — 3,000 bonus points per 3 nights, up to 36,000 points[14][15]

Registration window and stay window both September 29 – December 15, 2026. Register before completing your first eligible night. 3,000 bonus points per every 3 eligible nights stayed, nights don't need to be consecutive, award nights count. Max 12x repeats (36 nights total) for 36,000 bonus points. Participating Hyatt hotels and resorts worldwide, including Mr & Mrs Smith hotels, the Venetian Resort, and Homes & Hideaways. This resolves ledger call 2026-08-28-1 (HIT when graded) — Hyatt's Q4 earn-side push arrived as predicted and sits as one of the smaller Q4 bonus promos among the big chains.

register now if you have any Hyatt paid stays before Dec 15 — the only loss from registering is zero. The 3,000-points-per-3-nights structure makes this most valuable for members already running 9+ paid Hyatt nights this quarter; below 3 nights total you get nothing.
