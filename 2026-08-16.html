Today's Transfer Bonuses

Transfers are one-way and irreversible. Move points only with a specific redemption already priced and, where possible, held. Every figure below was checked against the program's own page — sources numbered at the foot.

Citi ThankYou → Air France/KLM Flying Blue — 20%

Expires 11:59 PM ET, Friday August 22, 2026 · 1,000 Citi = 1,200 Flying Blue · eligible cards: Strata Elite, Strata Premier, AT&T Access More[1][2]

The shortest fuse on the board — six days out. Flying Blue's US↔Europe business awards start at 60,000 miles one-way with promo pricing occasionally as low as 45,000. At the bonus, that 60k costs 50,000 Citi points.

Your move: transfer only if you have a specific Flying Blue seat identified on aircanada.com or the Flying Blue site with award space visible today. Do not park points speculatively — Flying Blue miles expire after 24 months of inactivity and Citi runs this same 20% promo roughly twice a year.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles — 40%

Expires 11:59 PM Türkiye time (UTC+3), Monday August 31, 2026 · 1,000 Citi = 1,400 Turkish · bonus capped at 50,000 bonus miles (max out at 125,000 Citi transferred) · bonus miles arrive on/after Sep 1[3][2]

The classic Turkish sweet spot — 45,000 miles round-trip in Star Alliance business between the continental US and Hawaii on United — costs 32,143 Citi points after the 40% bonus. That is one of the best premium-cabin redemptions in the entire points ecosystem.

Your move: only transfer if you have already called Turkish (+1-800-874-8875) and confirmed award availability on your date, and you accept that bonus miles land after Sep 1 — meaning any booking you need to make in August must be covered by the base transfer alone.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards — 70%

Expires 11:59 PM ET, Sunday August 31, 2026 · 1,000 Chase = 1,700 IHG[4][2]

Two weeks left. IHG points are worth roughly 0.5¢ apiece as a working average, so 1,700 IHG per 1,000 Chase gives about 0.85¢/UR — below the ~1.5–2¢/UR you can normally extract via Hyatt, Air Canada or a Sapphire Reserve travel redemption. This bonus is only compelling if you have an IHG stay already priced and it beats the cash rate.

Your move: skip unless you have an IHG award night priced today at a rate where 1,700 points beats the cash equivalent by 20% or more. This bonus rarely wins the maths against holding UR for Hyatt or Aeroplan.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Air Canada Aeroplan — 20%

Expires 11:59 PM ET, Wednesday September 30, 2026 · 1,000 Chase = 1,200 Aeroplan · stacks with a 10% Aeroplan-cardholder bonus (capped at 25,000 bonus/year on transfers ≥50,000)[5][2]

Aeroplan is one of the best award engines in the world — free stopovers on international itineraries for 5,000 points, a distance-based partner chart, and Star Alliance availability that United's own program often hides. The stackable Aeroplan-cardholder bonus makes this a rare "1,300 Aeroplan per 1,000 UR" moment.

Your move: transfer if you hold the Chase Aeroplan card AND have a specific Star Alliance itinerary identified — otherwise wait; you have six weeks and the discipline of "no speculative transfers" applies double when a stopover-eligible booking is worth building around.

Amex Membership Rewards → British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus Avios — 30%

Expires Sunday September 27, 2026 · 1,000 Amex = 1,300 Avios · one consolidated bonus because Avios transfer freely between the three programs[6][2]

Frequent Miler explicitly flags this as "no need to transfer speculatively right now" — you have six full weeks. Iberia off-peak transatlantic business (JFK/BOS/ORD/MIA↔MAD) at 34,000 Avios one-way is the classic redemption; at this bonus that is 26,154 Amex points.

Your move: keep points in Amex; only transfer once you have an Iberia off-peak seat held on the phone (+1-800-772-4642). The bonus runs long enough that speculative moves are strictly worse than waiting.

Capital One Miles → Avianca LifeMiles — 15%

August 1-31, 2026 · 1,000 Capital One = 1,150 LifeMiles · minimum 1,000-mile transfer, credited within 24 hours[7][2]

A modest bonus for a program with a real sweet spot: US↔Europe business on Star Alliance for 63,000 LifeMiles one-way, which the bonus brings to 54,783 Capital One miles. But LifeMiles expire after 12 months of inactivity — a live trap for anyone who transfers and defers.

Your move: skip unless you have booked award space on hold; 15% is a floor, not a ceiling. Avianca ran 25% and 30% bonuses in 2025 and will likely do so again before year-end.

Amex / Citi / Capital One / Bilt / Marriott → Cathay Pacific Asia Miles — 10%

Expires 11:59 PM PT, Friday August 28, 2026 · bonus cap 10,000 Asia Miles per member across all partners combined · registration required · Amex ratio is 5:4 base (not 1:1); max bonus at 125,000 Amex transferred · Marriott is 3:1[8][9]

A weak bonus on a still-useful currency, and the fine print matters more than the headline. From Amex, 1,000 MR becomes 880 Asia Miles after the 10% is applied on top of the 5:4 base — a poor conversion. From Citi, Capital One or Bilt at 1:1 base, 1,000 becomes 1,100 Asia Miles. The 10,000-mile cap makes this a bonus for topping off an award, not building a balance.

Your move: transfer only from a 1:1 source (Citi, Capital One or Bilt), only to top off a specific Cathay or oneworld award you have identified, and only up to the 10,000-mile cap. Do not transfer from Amex at this bonus rate.

Program Watch

U.S. Bank Altitude Reserve transfer partners: launched then two were pulled

On August 11, 2026, U.S. Bank finally shipped its long-promised transferable-points program on the Altitude Reserve, with Accor (2:1), Ethiopian ShebaMiles (1:1), Air France/KLM Flying Blue (1:1) and Qantas Frequent Flyer (1:1). Roughly 24 hours later, on August 12, Flying Blue and Qantas were removed from the transfer list — reported by Doctor of Credit, Frequent Miler and One Mile at a Time as an IT-integration failure rather than a deal collapse. The Altitude Reserve remains closed to new applicants; only existing cardholders have access.[10][11]

Your move: if you hold an Altitude Reserve, Ethiopian ShebaMiles is the sweet spot worth studying (US↔Europe business at 65,000 miles one-way on Star Alliance) — but do not transfer anything speculatively while U.S. Bank's implementation is this fresh. Wait for at least 30 days of stable operation.

Hyatt: premium card and Sapphire Reserve elite pathway coming

Following the May 20, 2026 award-chart restructure into five demand-based tiers (Lowest through Top), Hyatt and Chase have confirmed additional changes: at least one new premium World of Hyatt card is planned, and Chase Sapphire Reserve cardholders will earn World of Hyatt Explorist status at $75,000 in annual spend, with Sapphire Reserve for Business requiring $120,000.[12]

Your move: no action today; useful only for planning. If you are near either spend threshold on an existing Sapphire Reserve, the calculus for adding a Hyatt co-brand may change once terms drop — do not chase the spend on speculation.

Marriott 2026 devaluation continues to bite co-brand free-night certificates

The 50,000-point free-night certificate on Marriott co-brand cards increasingly falls short of a Category 5 property under the post-devaluation chart, with the ability to top off up to 25,000 points now the primary path to using them at desirable hotels.[13]

Your move: audit any 50k-anniversary certificates in your account and identify redemptions within 90 days of expiry — un-topped-off certificates now die on the vine at properties that used to accept them cleanly.
