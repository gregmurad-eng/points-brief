Today's Transfer Bonuses

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards

70% bonus — 1,000 Chase points become 1,700 IHG points. This is the tail end of a promo that ran 100% in mid-July before stepping down. IHG's sweet spots skew to InterContinental redemptions in Asia and Points & Cash on Kimpton stays; it is a program to transfer at booking, not to speculate on.

Expires August 31, 2026 · AwardWallet coverage

Amex Membership Rewards → British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus Avios

30% bonus — 1,000 MR points become 1,300 Avios. Because Avios flow freely between BA, Iberia, Aer Lingus, Finnair, and Qatar Privilege Club, this is effectively a 30% bonus to the whole Avios ecosystem. Short-haul American Airlines domestic on Avios remains the standout: 8,000–10,000 Avios for economy segments AA now prices at 15k+ SkyMiles-style.

Expires September 27, 2026 · LoyaltyLobby detail

Amex Membership Rewards (and hotel points) → Cathay Asia Miles

10% bonus — a modest bump on an already-poor 1:0.75 base ratio, meaning the effective rate is 0.825 Asia Miles per MR point. Requires registration on Cathay's site and is capped at 10,000 bonus miles per member. Only interesting if you have a specific Cathay First or Business award already on hold.

Expires August 28, 2026 · Upgraded Points detail

Capital One Miles → Avianca LifeMiles

15% bonus — 1,000 Capital One miles become 1,150 LifeMiles, minimum 1,000 mile transfer, no registration required. LifeMiles is a strong Star Alliance program — useful for United domestic short-hauls, Lufthansa First when it opens close-in, and Copa within Latin America.

Expires August 31, 2026 · Upgraded Points detail

Also worth registering

World of Hyatt Bonus Journeys

3,000 bonus points per every 3 nights, stacking up to 21,000 total, with additional brand-specific kickers. Registration is required. If you have Hyatt stays already on the calendar this month, this is essentially free points — nothing to transfer, just enroll.

United MileagePlus — Buy Miles up to 45% off

Rarely a good idea to buy speculatively, but with a confirmed premium-cabin award on hold this can pencil out at roughly 1.6¢ per mile all-in. Do the math against the cash fare before committing.

Today's Play

If you are holding a confirmed IHG award or a specific Avios itinerary, transfer now. If not, the honest signal for August 14 is do nothing: none of these bonuses are meaningfully better than the transfer partners' medians, and Chase-to-IHG at 70% will not be missed by anyone without an existing IC or Six Senses hold. The one exception is Hyatt Bonus Journeys — if you have Hyatt nights on the books, register today.

Reminder: transfers from Chase, Amex, and Capital One to airline and hotel partners are one-way and irreversible. Never move points speculatively. Confirm award-seat availability and total pricing in the destination program before initiating a transfer, and remember that award charts, taxes, and fuel surcharges can change without notice.
