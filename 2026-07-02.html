Lead Story

Chase Quietly Devalues Hyatt Transfers — And Draws a Line Between Its Sapphire Cards

The most valuable transfer partner in the Chase ecosystem just got more expensive to reach. Chase is cutting the World of Hyatt transfer ratio by 25% on the Sapphire Preferred, a change that reshapes the calculus for anyone who has leaned on cheap Hyatt redemptions as the crown jewel of Ultimate Rewards.

The timing splits cardholders into two camps. Anyone who applied for a Sapphire Preferred on or after June 15, 2026 is subject to the new, worse ratio immediately. Existing Preferred cardholders get a runway — the change does not hit their accounts until October 1, 2026. The escape hatch: the Chase Sapphire Reserve and Sapphire Reserve for Business retain full 1:1 transfers to Hyatt, quietly widening the gap between Chase's mid-tier and premium products.

For Greg's stack, the read is simple: if Hyatt is central to how you burn Chase points — think Park Hyatt aspirational stays or the sub-15k domestic sweet spots — the Reserve is now doing real, ongoing work that the Preferred no longer does. If you hold the Preferred, you have until October to move any planned Hyatt transfers at today's ratio. Full breakdown at TPG.

Today's Briefs

Hyatt's Summer Global Promo Is Live

World of Hyatt is running a Summer 2026 global promotion: 2,000 bonus points after every two eligible nights at any property worldwide, capped at 8,000 bonus points. Stays count July 1 through September 7, but you must register between June 22 and September 1 — and earning only begins with your second stay after registration. Details.

United's Earning Overhaul Is Now In Effect

The MileagePlus earning changes that took effect April 2 continue to bite: general members without a United co-branded card earn fewer miles on United-operated flights, while cardholders and elites earn more. If you're funneling United flying without a co-brand, the math for keeping one just improved. NerdWallet.

Hilton Points At A Discount, If You Need Them

The Amex-to-Hilton 20% bonus running all July is rarely a headline — Hilton points are worth roughly half a cent each — but for a specific, already-priced high-end redemption (a Waldorf or Conrad on points), topping off a balance at a 20% discount can pencil out. Don't speculatively transfer. Context.

Delta Holds Steady

No major SkyMiles devaluation this cycle — Delta continues its fully dynamic model with gradual partner tweaks rather than chart-wide cuts. For Greg's SkyMiles balance, that's a neutral-to-good signal: no urgent burn pressure, but no fixed sweet spots to chase either. AwardFares guide.
