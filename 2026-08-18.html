World of Hyatt 2026 category shifts now live[5][7]

Effective May 20, 2026 · Tier 1 (Hyatt newsroom) confirmed the new five-level structure; property-count numbers from Frequent Miler / Upgraded Points trade coverage

The 2026 chart moved from the old three-level (Off-Peak / Standard / Peak) structure to a five-level structure (Lowest / Low / Moderate / Upper / Top) inside each of the eight categories — Hyatt frames this as "more precise demand management" rather than a chart increase[7]. Live since June 2026 · Tier 2 trade confirmation; United's press page not independently fetched
