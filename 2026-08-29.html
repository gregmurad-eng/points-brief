Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards — 70%

Ratio: 1,000 UR = 1,700 IHG  ·  Expires: 2026-08-31 [3]

The headline number is loud; the underlying math is not. Chase steps down its own promo mid-run — 100% for the July 15–30 early window, 70% since — because IHG points routinely sell direct for around 0.5¢ each. Even at 1,700 IHG per 1,000 UR, you are effectively parking Chase points at roughly 0.85¢ apiece, well below the ~1.5–2¢ Chase points fetch through Hyatt, Aeroplan, or Avios.

skip unless you are covering an exact IHG award already priced out on ihg.com and the shortfall is under 20,000 IHG — for anything bigger, the calendar-quarter card renewal certificate or a direct IHG points purchase is cheaper than burning Chase points at this ratio.

Capital One miles → Avianca LifeMiles — 15%

Ratio: 1,000 Capital One = 1,150 LifeMiles  ·  Minimum: 1,000 miles in 100-mile increments  ·  Expires: 2026-08-31 [4]

Modest bonus, real utility. LifeMiles remains one of the cleanest Star Alliance award engines — no fuel surcharges on partner metal, distance-based pricing that keeps US-domestic short-haul on United at 12,500 economy / 25,000 business one-way, and US↔Europe business from 63,000 miles. The 15% is not the point; being able to top up a booking-ready account without waiting for a bigger promo is.

transfer only to complete a booking you can price and hold today — LifeMiles inventory is real-time and evaporates. Do NOT speculate: LifeMiles has run 20% and 25% bonuses within the last 12 months, and Capital One's Q4 push is the base case in October (see The Ledger).

Amex Membership Rewards → Avios (British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus) — 30%

Ratio: 1,000 MR = 1,300 Avios to any of the three  ·  Expires: 2026-09-27 [6]

The best-value bonus of the current cycle. Avios balances move freely between BA, Iberia, and Aer Lingus, so you pick the destination based on the redemption: Iberia for peak short-haul Madrid–Europe (from 9,000 Avios one-way in off-peak), Aer Lingus for Dublin transatlantic, BA for the AA short-haul sweet spot inside the US (9,000 Avios in economy, 15,000 in business for under 651-mile hops on AA). Point.me characterises this as the best offer live right now [7] and I agree with the framing.

transfer to whichever Avios wallet holds the specific award you plan to book — Iberia if the redemption is on IB metal (better taxes), BA if on AA or Qatar (broader partner tool), Aer Lingus if on EI. Do not transfer to a general "Avios stockpile" and figure it out later; each program's cash-fee structure on the same partner segment can differ by three figures.
