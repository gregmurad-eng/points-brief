Today's Transfer Bonuses

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy · 70%

Ratio 1:1.7 · through October 15, 2026 · no registration required · no announced cap

Chase's highest-ever transfer bonus to Marriott, running one month (Sep 15 – Oct 15).[1][2][3][7] 1,000 UR points become 1,700 Bonvoy points. The maths still isn't flattering — Marriott points value at roughly 0.7¢ each, so 1,700 Bonvoy is ~$11.90, versus 1,000 UR redeemed through Chase Travel at 1.5¢ (Sapphire Reserve) = $15.[7]

Your move: transfer only if you already have a specific Marriott award identified — ideally a fifth-night-free redemption at a Cat 6+ property that closes the value gap. Do not transfer speculatively; the 70% is a headline, not a mandate.

Amex Membership Rewards → Hilton Honors · 30%

Ratio 1:2.6 · through October 14, 2026

Amex's headline bonus for September/October.[4][5][6] 1,000 MR becomes 2,600 Hilton points. Hilton points value around 0.5¢, so 2,600 Hilton is worth roughly $13 — still one of Amex's weaker per-point transfer destinations even at this ratio.

Today's Play

Signal

The interesting spot in today's market is what isn't happening. Chase is running a record-breaking 70% Marriott bonus for one month, and neither Amex nor Citi has responded with a matching MR → Marriott or TY → Marriott offer. That silence is a signal, not an absence: the bank programs run to their own cadences and don't reactively undercut each other during co-invested hotel windows.

Practical read: if you were waiting on an Amex → Marriott bonus before topping up a Bonvoy balance, this window is unlikely to produce one. Either transfer via Chase now (with the caveats above), or wait for Amex's own semi-annual Marriott cadence, which historically lands in October or November on its own schedule. Today's ledger call bets explicitly on this holding.

Standing caution: every transfer is one-way and irreversible. Once points cross the wire from Chase, Amex, Citi, Capital One or Bilt to any airline or hotel partner, they cannot be moved back. Never transfer speculatively — always confirm award availability, cash-price alternatives, and change/cancel terms before initiating a transfer, and prefer holding a confirmed award space with a phone-held or ticketed reservation whenever the partner permits it.
