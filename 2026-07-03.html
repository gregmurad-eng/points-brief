Lead Story

Virgin Atlantic Gets a Double 30% — From Both Sides of Your Wallet

For the first time in a while, both of Greg’s flexible currencies are courting the same partner at the same rate. Chase Ultimate Rewards and Amex Membership Rewards are each running a 30% transfer bonus to Virgin Atlantic Flying Club. Move 1,000 points and 1,300 Virgin points land in your account.

Virgin’s Flying Club remains one of the sharpest sweet spots in the game — think ANA and Delta One partner space bookable through Virgin at a fraction of the retail mileage cost. The catch is timing: the two bonuses run on different clocks. Chase’s window closes July 14; Amex’s runs through July 31. If you have a specific redemption in view, price the award first, then transfer only what you need — points don’t come back. See the current terms at AwardWallet.
