Today's Transfer Bonuses

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards · 80% · through Dec 3

Transfer ratio 1:1.8 · 1,000-point minimum[1]

This remains the highest Chase-to-IHG bonus on record and is Chase's headline Q4 offer. IHG points historically redeem for about 0.5–0.6¢ each, so 1 UR point yields roughly 0.9–1.1¢ through the bonus — below what UR fetches through most airline transfers or Chase Travel's 1.25–1.5¢ portal rate for premium cardholders.

Your move: transfer only if you have a specific IHG redemption ≥ 0.6¢/point already identified (points-and-cash on a hard-to-get Kimpton or an InterContinental PointBreaks property). Otherwise skip — IHG points are a weak terminal store and UR has better exits.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club · 40% · through Dec 30

Transfer ratio 1:1.4 · 1,000-point minimum · bonus points posted up to 7 days after transfer[2]

Top-of-range for Chase-to-Virgin, and it's live simultaneously with the 30% Amex-to-Virgin bonus — an unusual alignment driven by Virgin Atlantic pushing transatlantic load factor. At 1:1.4, Virgin's ANA First Class (120k VS for round-trip from the US to Japan) effectively costs about 86k UR, and Delta One transatlantic awards (50k VS one-way) drop near 36k UR.

Your move: if you already hold an award on Virgin Atlantic — ANA First, Delta One, or Virgin's own Clubhouse Upper Class — transfer via Chase, not Amex. 40% beats 30%. Do not speculate into Flying Club without the seat first; VS award space is thin and the taxes on Virgin-metal flights are punishing.

Amex Membership Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club · 30% · through Nov 30

Transfer ratio 1:1.3 · 1,000-point minimum · US cardholders[4]

Amex's offer runs 29 days shorter than Chase's and at a worse rate. Only useful if your point balance is in MR, not UR, or if the seat you want cannot be funded in time via a Chase transfer window.

Your move: if you have both Chase and Amex sitting around, use Chase. If you're Amex-only with a confirmed Virgin award in hand before Nov 30, transfer the exact amount — no padding. Any spare MR stays put; another Amex-to-Virgin 30%+ bonus has historically returned within 4–6 months.

Amex Membership Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy · 35% · through Dec 10

Transfer ratio 1:1.35 · bonus hardcoded into transfer ratio · elite nights do NOT count[3]

Marriott Bonvoy points dropped to roughly 0.7¢ each after the mid-2026 program-wide devaluation[9]. At 1:1.35, each MR point yields about 0.95¢ through Bonvoy — still below the 1.1–1.5¢ MR gets via Flying Blue, ANA Mileage Club or Avios partners.

Your move: use this only to top off an existing Bonvoy balance for a specific 5-night award (where the 5th night comes free), and only if your remaining gap is small. Do not start from zero to Marriott via Amex — the math stays red.

Citi ThankYou Rewards → Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles · 40% · through Nov 30

Transfer ratio 1:1.4 · 50,000-bonus-mile cap (max out at 125,000 TY transferred)[5]

Turkish remains the Star Alliance sweet spot — 7,500 miles one-way intra-Europe in economy, 12,500 to Hawaii from the mainland US in economy, 45,000 US ↔ Europe in business on partner metal. At 1:1.4 that US-Hawaii award costs under 9,000 TY. The warning is operational: Turkish's booking portal and call-center reliability have been inconsistent, and transfers are irreversible.

Your move: transfer only if you've already confirmed award space exists through United.com or Aeroplan search AND you can get a human on the phone at Turkish to ticket it. Hitting the 50k cap requires moving 125k TY — don't do that unless you're planning multiple redemptions this year.

Capital One Miles → Air France/KLM Flying Blue · 20% · through Nov 30

Transfer ratio 1:1.2 · no registration required[6]

The weakest headline in today's stack by percentage, and Flying Blue runs monthly "Promo Rewards" that rotate sweet spots on their own. If your target isn't in this month's promo list, a 20% transfer-in bonus alone isn't a strong enough signal.

Your move: skip unless you've already identified a specific Promo Rewards route that aligns. A 20% bonus into dynamic FB pricing is a marginal win at best — Capital One miles also transfer 1:1 to Virgin Red, Air Canada Aeroplan, Avianca LifeMiles, and several other programs with better current exits.

Program Watch

Cathay Pacific Asia Miles — partner award chart devalues Jan 5, 2027

Cathay has confirmed Asia Miles rates on partner-airline redemptions will rise by up to 20% from 5 January 2027 across selected distance bands and cabin classes. First Class ultra-long-haul climbs from 170,000 to 204,000 miles; economy ultra-short jumps from 10,000 to 12,000. Companion Ticket Awards — the long-standing feature allowing paid Business/First travelers to book a companion with miles — will no longer be offered as of the same date. Standard Cathay-metal awards, upgrades, and oneworld Multi-Carrier awards are unchanged. Awards ticketed before Jan 5 can still have dates changed afterward without repricing.[7]

Your move: if you hold Asia Miles or transferrable points you intended to send to Cathay for a partner redemption (Qatar Qsuite, JAL, Finnair, Qantas), lock award space and ticket before Jan 5. Do NOT speculatively top off Asia Miles — a transfer-in bonus of 15%+ into a program announcing a rate hike is unlikely, and Companion Awards cannot be issued once the deadline passes.

American Airlines ends Hyatt Rewards partnership — effective Nov 15, 2026

The AAdvantage × World of Hyatt enhanced partnership — which allowed status matching between AA Platinum and Hyatt Explorist, and bonus mile/point earning on co-brand stays — ended on November 15, 2026[8]. Confirm individually whether any downstream perks you were relying on (such as status-match reciprocity or linked earning) are still honored before booking.

Your move: if your plan relied on AAdvantage × Hyatt crossover benefits for a Q1 2027 trip, requalify via a native path — Hyatt Explorist via 30 nights or qualifying paid stays, AA Platinum via Loyalty Points. Don't make irreversible transfers predicated on the old linkage.

Hyatt Milestone Rewards chart updated Nov 19, 2026

World of Hyatt added new Milestone Rewards: a $30 spa credit at Inclusive Collection resorts, a $200 Thompson Hotels credit, a $500 premium-suite booking credit, and free parking on award or paid stays up to 7 nights at applicable brands[10]. Milestones are earned at defined night thresholds (20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 100). This is additive, not a devaluation — but it's the structural piece that follows Hyatt's earlier 2026 award-chart reshuffle and signals Hyatt's intent to reward tenure in-kind rather than through additional points.

Your move: check your current night count in the Hyatt app. If you're within a handful of nights of a Milestone threshold worth more than paid stays that would get you there, favor Hyatt bookings between now and year-end. No points transfer action required.
