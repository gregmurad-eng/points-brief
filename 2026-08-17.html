Today's Transfer Bonuses

Citi ThankYou → Air France/KLM Flying Blue — 20% (1,000 : 1,200)

Expires August 22, 2026. Source: Citi ThankYou promo, triangulated across trade desks.[1][2]

The nearest expiry on the board. Flying Blue's promo awards (Promo Rewards) can be extraordinary — 30–50% off already-cheap partner economy on SkyTeam — but they change every month, so this bonus is only useful if you have a specific promo window in mind or a US–Europe business seat priced at 50,000–75,000 miles that you can actually book.

Your move: transfer only if you have a Flying Blue award already in your cart and confirmed available at the checkout page. Do not top up "for later" — Flying Blue devalues quietly and often, and Promo Rewards change monthly.

Multiple programs → Cathay Pacific Asia Miles — 10% (varies by source)

Expires August 28, 2026. From Amex MR (1,000:880 post-2:1.6 base ratio), Capital One (100:110), Citi (1,000:1,100), Bilt (1,000:1,100), Brex (3:2.2).[2][3]

A modest sweetener on an already-modest ratio (Amex→Asia Miles is 1,000:800 base). Asia Miles' sweet spots are Cathay-operated business/first Hong Kong–US, JAL Tokyo–US in J, and the Qantas Sydney–LAX F seat when you can find it — all of which require award space that has been thin all summer.

Your move: skip unless you can pull up a live Cathay/JAL business-class seat right now in Cathay's own award tool. A 10% bonus does not fix a bad base ratio if you have no award to book.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards — 70% (1,000 : 1,700)

Expires August 31, 2026. Minimum transfer 1,000 UR. Chase stepped this down from a 100% "early bird" (Jul 15–30).[3][4][5]

Optically the biggest number on the page, but the math is unfavourable for almost everyone. Chase UR is worth roughly 2.0¢/point on flexible-partner redemptions; IHG points are worth about 0.5¢. Even at 1.7 IHG per UR you are converting 2.0¢ into about 0.85¢ — a haircut of more than half. There is exactly one case where this works: you are 500–3,000 IHG short of a specific award night you are booking today, and no other redemption path exists.

Your move: skip unless you are topping up for a specific IHG award night already priced. Never transfer speculatively into IHG — the currency devalues faster than the bonus recovers.

Capital One Miles → Avianca LifeMiles — 15% (100 : 115)

Expires August 31, 2026.[2][3]

Small bonus, meaningful currency. LifeMiles still hold two of the last durable Star Alliance sweet spots — 63,000 miles one-way in business to Europe on Lufthansa/Swiss/United, and 78,000 to North Asia. LifeMiles also charges no fuel surcharges on partner awards. The 15% bonus barely moves the price, but it is a useful nudge if you are close on a specific booking.

Your move: transfer only against a held or confirmed Star Alliance business award. Confirm the partner space in United's tool first — LifeMiles' own search engine is notoriously flaky and will show phantom availability.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Miles & Smiles — 40% (1,000 : 1,400)

Expires August 31, 2026 at 23:59 Türkiye time (16:59 ET) — treat this as an Aug 31 lunchtime deadline in the US.[2][6][7]

The most legitimately interesting expiring offer. Turkish's US–Europe business at 45,000 miles one-way on Star Alliance partners, and their 7,500-mile domestic-US United economy award, remain two of the best sweet spots in the entire ecosystem. But Turkish is famously hard to actually book — phone bookings, chat agents who disappear, and web availability that shows partners inconsistently. The 40% bonus is best pre-transferred against a specific reservation you have already confirmed by phone.

Your move: only worth it if you have already spoken to a Turkish agent (call the US line) and had a partner award held. Do not transfer to Turkish "to see" — points that land in Miles & Smiles are stuck there, and Turkish's chart could revalue without notice.

Amex Membership Rewards → British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus Avios — 30% (1,000 : 1,300)

Expires 11:59pm MT on September 27, 2026. Amex ran this from Aug 3.[2][8][9]

The best-value bonus running right now for most US points holders. Avios is a pooled currency across the three programs, so you can transfer to whichever has the best redemption chart for your route — Iberia for Madrid non-stops in business (34,000 Avios off-peak), Aer Lingus for Dublin, BA for the short-haul AA-operated hops at 4,500 Avios each (still one of the few real sweet spots left in the domestic US after BA's December 2025 devaluation of longer AA awards).[10] At 1,300 Avios per 1,000 MR, that 4,500-Avios AA hop costs roughly 3,462 MR.

Your move: worth speculative-ish transferring in 5,000–10,000 MR increments if you fly AA short-haul or plan a Europe trip in the next 12 months. Iberia specifically is the target for a Madrid off-peak business seat. Skip if you have no near-term Europe or AA short-haul plan — Avios devalue too.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Air Canada Aeroplan — 20% (1,000 : 1,200)

Expires September 30, 2026. Some cardholders may see a targeted 30% (1:1.3) rate in-app.[3][11]

Aeroplan devalued 32 partner-award prices on June 1, 2026, so the chart is worse than it was a year ago[12] — but the underlying program is still one of the more flexible ways to book Star Alliance business, and stopovers-for-5K remain intact. A 20% bonus is a below-average Chase→Aeroplan offer (they have run 30% and 40% versions in prior years), so this is not a bonus to burn all your UR on.

Your move: check your Chase transfer page for a targeted 30% rate before moving anything at 20%. If you only see 20% and have a specific Aeroplan award in mind, transfer against it. Otherwise wait — Chase runs Aeroplan bonuses roughly twice a year and a better rate is plausible before year-end.

Today's Play

If you hold Amex Membership Rewards and fly American Airlines domestic short-haul (under 650 miles one-way), the Amex → Avios 30% bonus is the single most defensible transfer running today. The math: a 4,500-Avios AA hop (LAX–PHX, DCA–BOS, MIA–ATL, etc.) costs about 3,462 MR with the bonus — versus $150–$300 in cash. Even at Amex's conservative 1.8¢/point valuation, that is a 2¢+ return, before taxes and fees.

The Iberia sub-play: an off-peak Madrid business seat is 34,000 Avios one-way. With the bonus, that is roughly 26,200 MR. Non-peak dates through spring 2027 have real availability.

Not doing anything today is also a play. The 30% bonus runs six more weeks — no rush to transfer before you have a specific booking.
