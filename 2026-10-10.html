Today’s Transfer Bonuses

Citi ThankYou → Qatar Airways Privilege Club — up to 35% (tiered)[1][2][3]

Base ratio 1:1 · promo runs October 1–31, 2026 · bonus posts by November 30, 2026 · tiered: 15% bonus on single transfers up to 49,999 Avios, 25% bonus on 50,000–99,999 Avios, 35% bonus on 100,000+ Avios transferred in a single transaction

This is a Qatar-side promotion, not a Citi-side one — which is why Citi’s transfer screen will not display the bonus during the transfer itself. Qatar credits the bonus Avios after the fact. At the 35% tier, 100,000 Citi ThankYou points becomes 135,000 Qatar Avios. Qatar Avios is a Avios-family currency that is pooled with British Airways, Iberia, and Aer Lingus balances, and the strongest redemption targets are Qatar’s own QSuite business class, as well as oneworld partner sweet spots including American Airlines domestic short-haul.[3]

Your move: Only transfer in blocks of 100,000+ Avios to earn the 35% tier — smaller transfers at 15% or 25% are not worth burning your Citi balance on. And only transfer if you have a specific QSuite route and date range in mind with award space held, since this is a one-way move and Qatar’s premium-cabin availability is tight.

1,000 Chase → 1,700 Marriott · expires 11:59 PM ET October 15, 2026 (five days) · base ratio 1:1 · no minimum · unlimited transfers

Your move: Transfer only if you have a specific Marriott award night priced at 50k Bonvoy or higher and have confirmed availability on your travel dates. If you don’t have a stay identified by Monday afternoon, let the bonus close — this corridor runs again, usually within 4–6 months.

Today’s Play

Signal: Qatar at 35% is the sharpest-angle use of Citi ThankYou on the board — but only above the 100k-Avios tier

Chase to Marriott 70% is louder and Amex to Hilton 30% is closer to expiry, but structurally the most interesting opportunity on the current board is the Citi to Qatar tiered bonus. The 35% top tier only triggers on single transfers of 100,000 Avios or more, which means you are moving at least 100,000 Citi ThankYou points in one go — a meaningful commitment. In return you get 135,000 Qatar Avios, enough for a one-way QSuite business-class seat on many Qatar routes from the US to the Middle East or onward to Africa, Asia, or Europe (price depends on route and date).

What makes this different from the Marriott or Hilton bonuses: Qatar’s premium-cabin availability is tight. If you don’t already have a QSuite route and date in mind with space held, treat this bonus as if it does not exist. If you do, it is the only US-bank route to Qatar Avios at an elevated rate, and the pooled Avios balance across the family makes the transferred points more flexible after the fact than any single-airline redemption suggests.
