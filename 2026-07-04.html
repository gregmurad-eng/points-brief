Lead Story

Virgin Atlantic is having a moment — and both your card stacks feed it

For a brief window, Virgin Atlantic Flying Club is sitting at the center of two simultaneous 30% transfer bonuses. Chase Ultimate Rewards is offering a 30% bonus through July 7, moving points at 1:1.3, while American Express Membership Rewards runs its own 30% bonus through July 31 at the same elevated rate. Both transfer instantly, which is unusual for a hotel-or-airline sweet spot this rich.

Why it matters for a stack like yours: Virgin Flying Club is the quiet backdoor to partner premium cabins — ANA first and business class from the US to Japan, Delta One transcontinental, and Virgin's own upgraded Upper Class. At 1.3 miles per point transferred, aspirational redemptions that normally feel out of reach get materially cheaper. The catch is the mismatch in deadlines: Chase's version is the one on the clock. If you have a specific award in mind and the miles need to land in Virgin, the Chase transfer is the one to decide on this weekend.
