Lead Story

Virgin Atlantic is having a moment — and both your wallets can play

For a brief window, both Chase and Amex are running 30% transfer bonuses to Virgin Atlantic Flying Club at the same time. Chase Ultimate Rewards runs through July 14; Amex Membership Rewards runs all the way through July 31. That overlap matters because Flying Club is one of the most efficient ways to book premium cabins on partners — ANA first and business class, Delta One transcon, and Air France/KLM — often for a fraction of what those partners charge directly. With a 30% bump, a 50,000-point transfer becomes 65,000 Flying Club miles.

The catch worth remembering: Virgin's award chart has real sweet spots but also steep taxes on some partners, so price the specific redemption before you move points. Transfers are irreversible. See the current landscape at Frequent Miler and The Points Guy.
