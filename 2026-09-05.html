Today's Transfer Bonuses

Seven active bank-transfer promotions on the board this morning. None expires within 72 hours, but the calendar is stacking up: five of the seven close by the end of September, and the most valuable single deadline for anyone with a Chase Sapphire Preferred is separately covered under Program Watch.

Amex Membership Rewards → Avios (British Airways, Iberia, Aer Lingus): 30%[1][2]

Standard 1,000 : 1,000 becomes 1,000 : 1,300 through 11:59 p.m. MT on September 27, 2026. All three Avios pools sync between BA, Iberia and Aer Lingus after transfer, so you can pick your issuing program based on which one has award space held. Bonus applies to US Amex cardmembers only; minimum 1,000-point transfer, 1,000-point increments.

Your move: transfer only if you have Iberia short-haul intra-Europe space held (17,000 Avios one-way in off-peak business is still the sweet spot), or an American Airlines domestic short-haul (single-partner) redemption on the calendar. Speculative transfers into the Avios wallet three weeks before Q4 devaluation season are not a good idea.

Amex Membership Rewards → Hilton Honors: 30%[3][4]

Standard 1,000 : 2,000 becomes 1,000 : 2,600 through October 14, 2026. This is the widest-runway offer on the board — nearly six weeks left. It may be targeted; check your Amex account. No stated cap.

Your move: ignore unless you already have a specific Hilton redemption in mind and the cash rate on that stay produces a per-point return above 0.5¢. Hilton points are Amex's lowest-value hotel destination in typical use, and a 30% bonus at a 1:2.6 ratio still lands you at MR points valued around 1.4¢ on a Hilton redemption — worse than what MR can earn elsewhere unless the specific property inflates the value.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Air Canada Aeroplan: 20% (30% for Aeroplan cardholders on 50K+ transfers)[5][6]

Base ratio 1,000 : 1,200 through September 30, 2026 for all eligible Chase UR cardholders. Holders of the Aeroplan co-branded credit card stack an additional 10% when transferring 50,000 or more points in a single transfer, capped at 25,000 bonus miles annually. Eligible source cards: Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Preferred, Ink Business Preferred, Ink Business Reserve, J.P. Morgan Reserve.

Your move: only transfer with a specific Aeroplan redemption held. The sweet spots that make Aeroplan matter — partner short-haul at 6,000 miles, transatlantic partner business under 60,000 — are still there and unchanged by the Blackstone investment settlement, but they are also space-dependent. If you do not have an award already selected on aircanada.com, keep the points in Chase.

Capital One Miles → JAL Mileage Bank: 30%[7][8]

Base Capital One → JAL ratio is 4:3 (750 JAL per 1,000 Cap One), so the 30% bonus lifts the effective ratio to 1,000 : 975 through September 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. ET. Minimum 1,000-mile transfer. Note: JAL requires accounts to be 60 days old before accepting transfers, only permits award booking for immediate family, and expires JAL miles after 36 months.

Your move: the JAL sweet spot is Emirates first-class awards priced in JAL miles (roughly half of Emirates' own rate) and JAL's own business-class round-trip pricing to Tokyo. If neither is on your calendar in the next 12 months, do nothing — a 3-year expiration clock is not a place to park speculative miles.

Citi ThankYou → Leading Hotels of the World Leaders Club: 25%[9][10]

Strata Premier, Strata Elite, Prestige and AT&T Access More cardholders get 1,000 : 250 through 11:59 p.m. ET on September 19, 2026 (two weeks left, no cap on repeat use). Other Citi cards get a worse rate (0.175 Leaders Club Points per ThankYou point).

Rove Miles → Copa Airlines ConnectMiles: 40% (launch bonus)[11][12]

Program Watch

Three structural changes are on the near-term calendar, and one of them lands in 25 days.

Chase Sapphire Preferred → Hyatt drops to 4:3 on October 1, 2026[14][15]

The largest structural change in Chase's transfer stack in years. From October 1, existing Sapphire Preferred cardholders and Ink Business Preferred cardholders will transfer UR to Hyatt at a 4:3 ratio — 40,000 UR points become 30,000 Hyatt points, a 25% cut. New applicants on or after June 15, 2026 already face the reduced ratio. The 1:1 ratio remains intact only for Chase Sapphire Reserve and Sapphire Reserve for Business cardholders. This is the first time Chase has ever reduced its Hyatt transfer ratio and it directionally reinforces call 2026-08-22-1 in the ledger — Chase is moving away from Hyatt as a general benefit, not toward a bonus on it.

Your move: if you hold Chase Sapphire Preferred (or Ink Business Preferred) AND you have a specific Hyatt redemption within your realistic 24-month planning horizon, transfer at 1:1 before September 30. If you hold Sapphire Reserve you are unaffected and should do nothing. Do not speculatively transfer to Hyatt without a real redemption in mind — Hyatt points do not expire from earn activity but they cannot come back out.

Flying Blue Light / Standard / Flex tiered awards take effect September 8, 2026[16][17]

Three days out. Air France and KLM direct awards will split into Light (no lounge / no changes / no refunds), Standard (adds lounge and flexibility) and Flex (reduced cash fees) tiers. Transatlantic business examples: Light 60,000 miles, Standard 75,000, Flex 110,000. Elite members retain benefits regardless of tier. Air France / KLM state the change "applies exclusively to travel on Air France and KLM, and not to travel on partner airlines, where pricing remains unchanged" — that language is the whole basis of ledger call 2026-09-02-1 betting Flying Blue keeps partner pricing intact through January 31, 2027.

Your move: if you are booking an AF/KLM direct award for travel departing on or after September 8 that you can lock in over the weekend, do it now under the current pre-tier structure. If your travel is via a SkyTeam partner or Etihad/GOL/Kenya, this change does not touch your booking.

Marriott Bonvoy Q4 2026 registration opens September 9, 2026 (leaked)[18]

LoyaltyLobby's leaked terms: registration opens September 9, stay window September 22 – November 22, 1,500 bonus points for each paid stay of 2+ nights, plus an additional 3,000 bonus points at resort properties (up to 4,500 per stay total). Award stays and certificate stays are ineligible. Not yet confirmed on marriott.com — the ledger's 2026-08-31-1 call bets Marriott launches by September 22 with materially matching terms.

Your move: if you have any Marriott paid stays on the calendar between September 22 and November 22, register the moment the promo goes live on September 9. If you do not, the promo is not a reason to book.
