Today’s Transfer Bonuses

Three transfer taps are wide open this week, but two of the headline offers reward you only if you already have an award in sight. The point stays the point: transfers are one-way. Below are the US-cardholder bonuses that are live today, ranked by actionable value, not by headline percentage.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club — 40%

Ends 11:59 PM ET Tuesday, December 30, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 UR = 1,400 Virgin points · Minimum transfer 1,000 points[1][2]

This is the week’s most useful offer for US cardholders with a specific redemption lined up. Virgin Atlantic Flying Club is a thin partner list but a deep one: ANA business class to Japan (115,000 Virgin points round-trip), Delta One on partner-award space, Virgin’s own Upper Class from the US East Coast to London (around 100,000 points one-way plus cash surcharges). Every one of those needs live partner-award space held before you transfer. Chase Ultimate Rewards points move to Virgin Atlantic instantly in most cases.

Transfer only if you already have an ANA or Virgin award on hold. If you’re speculating on future space, wait — this offer runs five weeks and Virgin Atlantic rarely releases ANA space more than a few weeks out.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards — 80%

Ends 11:59 PM ET Thursday, December 3, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 UR = 1,800 IHG points · Minimum transfer 1,000 points[3][4]

The headline number of the month, and the most dangerous. IHG One Rewards uses dynamic pricing with no award chart, so 1.8 IHG points per UR point only pays off if a specific property’s cash rate divided by its award rate beats the implied 0.5 cents per UR point you’re giving up. For most properties it doesn’t. Independent analysis from Frequent Miler explicitly warns that even at 80%, booking the same room through a travel-portal UR redemption is often the better math.

Transfer only if you’ve already priced a specific IHG stay and confirmed the implied value clears 0.6 cpp. Default answer here is: don’t.

Citi ThankYou Rewards → Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles — 40%

Ends 11:59 PM Turkey time (UTC+3) Monday, November 30, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 Citi = 1,400 Turkish miles · Bonus capped at 50,000 bonus miles (max efficient transfer = 125,000 Citi ThankYou points)[5][6]

Turkish Miles&Smiles remains the cleanest Star Alliance sweet spot in the market: business class to Europe at 45,000 miles one-way, US domestic on United at 10,000 miles short-haul economy, Hawaii at 12,500 miles one-way economy. Two timing warnings worth repeating: the bonus posts after the campaign ends (first week of December, not immediately), and the 50,000 bonus-mile cap means transferring more than 125,000 Citi ThankYou points in this window wastes the uplift.

If you have a named Turkish redemption in mind, transfer up to but not above 125,000 Citi points before Monday. If you don’t, skip — Turkish devaluations have been frequent, and a speculative Miles&Smiles balance ages badly.

Amex Membership Rewards → Virgin Atlantic Flying Club — 30%

Ends 11:59 PM MST Monday, November 30, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 MR = 1,300 Virgin points · Transfers in multiples of 1,000[7]

Stackable in practice with the Chase Virgin Atlantic promo: if you need to top off a Virgin Atlantic balance ahead of an award booking, you can move from both issuers this week. The Amex bonus credits automatically, no registration. Bonus Virgin points may take up to 72 hours to post.

Only transfer MR to Virgin Atlantic against a specific held award — otherwise wait for the next Amex bonus. (See today’s ledger entry for when that is likely to arrive.)

Amex Membership Rewards → Marriott Bonvoy — 35%

Ends Thursday, December 10, 2026 · Transfer ratio 1,000 MR = 1,350 Marriott points[8]

Marriott Bonvoy quietly devalued across roughly 95 percent of its portfolio in 2026, pushing average per-point value to about 0.7 cents, down from 0.8 the year before.[9] Even with a 35% uplift, MR to Marriott pencils at roughly 0.95 cents per MR point — well below what MR usually returns through airline partners. The rare exception: a specific Category 7 or 8 Marriott redemption you’ve already identified.

Do not transfer MR to Marriott speculatively. Transfer only against a named Bonvoy redemption whose cash equivalent exceeds the point cost at 0.95 cents.

Bilt Rewards adds Southwest Rapid Rewards (and JetBlue TrueBlue in October)

Southwest added November 2026; JetBlue added October 20, 2026; both 1:1[13]

Bilt now has 27 travel partners after JetBlue’s October add, with Southwest arriving this month. Both transfer 1:1. Southwest is the more mechanically useful add for US-based Bilt members: Rapid Rewards is revenue-based and Southwest cash fares price predictably against points, so the transfer value is easy to calculate.

If you’re holding Bilt points and have a Southwest flight on the horizon, price it both ways — Bilt transfers to Rapid Rewards at 1:1 and the US Companion Pass math changes for some flyers at this exchange rate.

Warning: the 40% Amex → Hilton Honors bonus making the rounds is UK-only

Bonus period September 29 to November 24, 2026 (UK Amex program only)[14]

Coverage of this bonus is circulating today because it is the first 40% Amex-to-Hilton promotion since 2022 and ends today in the UK. Multiple US aggregators have confirmed it is not running on US Amex Membership Rewards. If you see the headline and move to transfer, you will receive the standard 1 MR = 2 Hilton ratio, not 1 MR = 2.8. Confirm the promotion is visible in your own Amex account before transferring — this is the exact mistake that makes transfer bonuses hazardous.

Do not transfer MR to Hilton today expecting a 40% uplift. If you’re a US cardholder, the next US Hilton bonus is historically months away.

Today’s Play

The decision today is boring, which is the point. Four of this week’s bonuses expire November 30 (Amex → Virgin Atlantic, Citi → Turkish, Capital One → Flying Blue, Chase → Avios). None expires inside 72 hours, so the urgency is editorial, not real. Spend the next three days doing the only thing that makes a transfer bonus worth anything: price out and hold the actual award you’d book, before you move the points. If you can’t find the space, the right move is to not transfer — an uplifted balance you can’t redeem is just points you can’t move back.

The one exception where transferring without live space is defensible is Chase → Virgin Atlantic for a known ANA business redemption you plan to search for in the next two weeks, because Virgin Atlantic’s standard award cost on ANA is predictable and the 40% uplift is unlikely to return before mid-2027.
