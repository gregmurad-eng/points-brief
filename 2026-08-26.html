Today's Transfer Bonuses

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards · 70%

Ratio 1:1.7 · Expires August 31, 2026 · Confirmed on IHG promo page

Chase's stepped-down IHG promo has five days to run before it closes at 11:59 pm ET on August 31. The bonus is the same 70% we've seen since July 31 — the 100% early-bird tier ended a month ago. IHG points have thin real-world value (Frequent Miler and One Mile at a Time both peg them around 0.5 cents), so even a bonused 1:1.7 rate is roughly break-even against most Chase Travel redemption paths.[1][2]

Your move: transfer only if you have a specific IHG cash rate in front of you priced at 1.5 cents per point or higher (typically a Regent, Six Senses or Intercontinental award-night stay you're ready to book today). Do not speculate into an IHG balance — the base rate reverts to 1:1 on September 1 and IHG points have no meaningful liquidity out.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Air Canada Aeroplan · 20%

Ratio 1:1.2 · Expires September 30, 2026 · Confirmed on Chase transfer page

Aeroplan remains Chase's one live airline handshake. A 20% bonus makes single-partner Star Alliance business class from North America to Europe (60,000 base Aeroplan miles at the low band, so ~50,000 Chase points after the bump) genuinely competitive against Turkish at 65,000. Aeroplan's advantage is straightforward award routing and no surprise fuel surcharges on most partners; its disadvantage is thin premium-cabin space on the routes you probably want.[3]

Your move: transfer only against a held Aeroplan reservation or a specific saver-level flight showing bookable space in ExpertFlyer / Seats.aero right now. If you're between Aeroplan and Turkish for a US→Europe business ticket, hold the seat with Aeroplan (which has better cancellation flexibility) before transferring.

Amex Membership Rewards → British Airways, Iberia, Aer Lingus Avios · 30%

Ratio 1:1.3 · Expires September 27, 2026 · Confirmed on Amex transfer page

Amex's semi-annual Avios promo is halfway through. Transferred Avios can be shuffled between BA, Iberia, Aer Lingus, Finnair and Qatar at no cost inside the Avios family, so the destination question is mostly about redemption tail: Iberia short-haul to Madrid (17,000 off-peak) and BA short-haul on AA (6,500 domestic short-haul off-peak) remain the two mechanical sweet spots. Increments are 1,000 MR minimum.[4][5]

Your move: transfer only if you already know which specific Avios sweet spot you're playing for — most commonly, a JFK→MAD Iberia flight priced at 34,000 Avios in business, or a specific short-haul AA award. Do not speculate into an Avios balance a month before booking; British Airways surcharges on BA metal will eat the bonus.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Airlines Miles & Smiles · 40%

Ratio 1:1.4 · Expires August 31, 2026 (11:59 pm Türkiye time) · Cap 50,000 bonus miles · Bonus posts after campaign ends

Citi's Turkish promo is the standout percentage of the month and the deepest sweet-spot arbitrage on the board: 45,000 Miles & Smiles gets you a partner-airline business class one-way from North America to Europe on the Star Alliance chart Turkish hasn't touched since December 2025. At a bonused 1:1.4, that's just under 32,150 Citi ThankYou points. Two caveats: bonus miles credit only after the campaign closes, and the 50,000 bonus mile cap means transfers above ~125,000 Citi points earn base rate on the excess.[6][7]

Your move: transfer only if you have already confirmed Star Alliance saver-level business class space on a Turkish partner (United, Air Canada, Lufthansa, Swiss, ANA) and can hold it while your bonus miles post — Turkish routinely takes weeks to fix booking issues and its call centre is famously hard. If you cannot pull up specific space today, wait for the next Q4 promo.

Citi ThankYou → Leading Hotels of the World Leaders Club · 25%

Ratio 1,000:250 (Premium tier cards) or 1,000:175 (Standard tier) · Expires September 19, 2026 (11:59 pm ET)

Citi's second LHW bonus of 2026 (the last ran April 19 through May 16). At the Premium-tier ratio this is a poor mathematical trade — 1,000 ThankYou points → 250 Leaders Club points, and Leaders Club values inconsistently even at brand hotels. LHW's real value is in the "Bellini Club" status match paths and confirmed suite upgrades at member properties, not in accumulating a large point balance.[9][10]

Your move: transfer only if you already hold Bellini Club status or an equivalent LHW recognition path and can name the property you'll book. For most readers this is a no — Turkish is a materially better home for the same Citi points, and the 40% bonus on Turkish is a mechanical 60% better trade.

Capital One Miles → Avianca LifeMiles · 15%

Ratio 1:1.15 · Expires August 31, 2026 (11:59 pm ET) · No cap · No registration

The month's quietest headline promo — 15% is the low end of Capital One's typical LifeMiles range (Q2 ran 25%). LifeMiles still price Star Alliance business class US→Europe at 63,000, so a bonused 1:1.15 puts that transfer cost at ~54,800 Capital One miles, meaningfully better than Aeroplan's 60,000 baseline. LifeMiles has no fuel surcharges on Star partners and includes segments Aeroplan can't book.[11]

Your move: transfer only if you're within 10 days of ticketing a specific Star Alliance award and Capital One is your only viable source. The 15% is not compelling enough to speculate — historical cadence puts a 20-25% Capital One→LifeMiles bonus in Q4 as the base case, and my August 23 ledger call bets on exactly that.

Multi-partner → Cathay Pacific Asia Miles · 10%

Ratio 1:1.1 · Expires August 28, 2026 · Cap 10,000 bonus miles per member · Registration required before transfer

The last two days of Cathay's Q3 push, and the tightest terms Cathay has offered a US audience in years — 10% (down from 20% in Q4 2025 and 30% in Q4 2024), plus the 10k mile cap, plus mandatory registration. The bonus applies to transfers from Amex MR, Citi ThankYou, Capital One, Bilt and select hotel partners (Marriott Bonvoy is explicitly excluded from the bonus, though the base transfer still works). Asia Miles' sweet spot remains partner business class within Asia and the occasional Cathay First availability.[8]

Your move: transfer only if you have already registered for the promo AND have a specific Cathay or Oneworld award in mind. If you're just building a balance, wait — my ledger call today bets Cathay's next promo will not exceed today's generosity, but you gain nothing by pre-loading at a base 1:1 rate before then.

Program Watch

American Airlines · Elite complimentary upgrades capped at Premium Economy on 9 transcon/Hawaii routes (effective yesterday, August 25, 2026)

As of yesterday, AAdvantage elite members holding a Main Cabin ticket can no longer skip directly to Business on nine premium routes; the complimentary upgrade now lands in Premium Economy instead. Affected routes reported: JFK–LAX, JFK–SFO, BOS–LAX (premium transcon) and DFW–HNL/OGG/KOA, PHX–HNL, ORD–HNL/OGG (Hawaii widebody). Systemwide Upgrade certificates still clear economy directly to Business. To reach Business as a complimentary upgrade, elites now need to buy Premium Economy up front.[12][13]

Your move: if you regularly fly one of the nine listed routes as an Executive Platinum or higher, factor in the roughly $150–$400 Premium Economy upcharge on future ticket purchases — that's now the true price of your realistic upgrade path. On any other AA route, nothing changes.

World of Hyatt · May 2026 category shift still shaping Q4 planning

May 20's category reshuffle moved 112 properties up a tier, and the top-band Category 8 rate went 45,000 → 75,000 points per night — a 67% jump at the ceiling. Mid-tier Cat 1-4 properties took 17-25% increases at off-peak and standard rates. This is not new news, but it is the change most affecting current Q4 award planning, and it's why Chase→Hyatt still has never carried a transfer bonus (my August 22 ledger call sits on that).[14]

Your move: if you are holding Chase points against an aspirational Hyatt redemption at a Category 7-8 property, model the trip at the new price before transferring. Cat 1-4 properties remain the strongest Hyatt trade even after the reshuffle.

Amex Platinum · $895 annual fee active on all renewals from January 2, 2026

The Platinum refresh has now been in the field for almost eight months. Existing cardholders hitting their first renewal on or after January 2, 2026 pay $895 (up from $695, a 28% increase). New benefits stack claims cover the delta if you actively use them; a passive holder is worse off. Chase Sapphire Reserve preceded the move in fall 2025 with a $795 AF on renewals after October 26, 2025.[15][16]

Your move: if your Platinum renewal is coming up in the next 60 days, run the credits math against your actual behaviour — most readers are downgrading to Gold or product-changing rather than absorbing the fee. If you hold both Platinum and CSR passively, one of them almost certainly no longer belongs in the wallet.
