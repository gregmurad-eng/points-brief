Today's Transfer Bonuses

Cathay Pacific Asia Miles — 10% bonus, multi-bank[1][2]

Ends 28 August 2026, 11:59pm PT · Amex MR (5:4), Citi ThankYou (1:1), Capital One (1:1), Bilt (1:1), Wells Fargo (1:1), Brex (1:1) · Marriott Bonvoy (3:1) · Registration required · Cap: 10,000 bonus miles combined

Cathay's Q3 partner push, registered through Cathay directly rather than the banks. The Amex ratio at 5:4 makes MR effectively a 45% haircut before the 10% bonus is applied — a losing arithmetic even with the kicker. Bilt, Capital One, Citi and Wells Fargo transfer 1:1, so the 10% bonus is real value there. Bonus miles post within 12 weeks of the transfer, so this is not usable for urgent redemptions.

Transfer only if you hold a specific Cathay or partner (JAL, Qatar) award you can't book more cheaply elsewhere, and only from a 1:1 program. Skip entirely from Amex. Register at Cathay's page before you transfer, or the bonus won't post.

Citi ThankYou → Turkish Miles & Smiles — 40% bonus[3][4]

Ends 31 August 2026, 23:59 Türkiye time (UTC+3 — earlier than US midnight) · 1,000:1,400 · Cap: 50,000 bonus miles (transfer 125,000 to max)

The strongest offer on the board by redemption math. Turkish Miles & Smiles still prices Star Alliance business class North America → Europe at 45,000 miles one-way (round-trip 90,000) on partners like United, Lufthansa, LOT and SWISS. With the 40% bonus that costs 32,150 Citi ThankYou points one-way — under 65,000 for round-trip business to Europe. Fuel surcharges are minimal on most Star partners; award availability is the constraint, not price.

Two catches worth knowing: the bonus is processed by Turkish after the campaign closes (so bonus miles won't be in your account when you transfer), and Turkish's award-search interface is famously unreliable — always confirm space by calling before you transfer.

If you have Star Alliance award space already held or verified over the phone, transfer to Turkish this week. If you're transferring speculatively, don't — Turkish's history of program changes without notice has burned speculative balances more than once.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → IHG One Rewards — 70% bonus[5][6]

Ends 31 August 2026 · Base ratio 1:1 (bonus makes it 1,000:1,700) · Requires Chase Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve, or Ink Business Preferred

The stepped-down phase of a two-part promo that ran at 100% July 15–30, then dropped to 70% for August. The 100% window was one of the highest UR→hotel bonuses Chase has ever offered; 70% is still generous by historical standards. IHG's own dynamic pricing means value depends heavily on the property — Intercontinental Bora Bora, Six Senses, and Kimpton Sylvan (Nashville) tend to offer real cents-per-point value; Holiday Inn Express in a suburban US market usually does not.

Transfer only if you have a specific IHG redemption in mind at over 0.75 cents/point in value — check IHG's cash price against the points cost before moving. This is the third time Chase has run an IHG bonus above 50% in the last two years, so a repeat within six months is unlikely but not impossible.

Ends 31 August 2026 · 100:115 · No cap disclosed

The weakest of the four expiring offers. LifeMiles' distance-based Star Alliance chart is still one of the strongest reachable from Capital One (63,000 US→Europe business, no fuel surcharges), but 15% is below the historical median.

Wait. If you need LifeMiles for a specific award right now, the 15% is fine — but if you're topping up speculatively, hold for the Q4 promo that Capital One's cadence strongly suggests is coming.

Amex Membership Rewards → BA, Iberia, Aer Lingus Avios — 30% bonus[8][9]

Ends 27 September 2026, 11:59pm MT · 1,000:1,300 · Min 1,000-point transfers

Routine Amex promo, run roughly semi-annually. Value hides in the specific Avios sweet spots: Iberia Madrid → Boston/New York/Chicago at 34,000 Avios off-peak in business (or ~26,000 Amex points post-bonus), Aer Lingus business class transatlantic starting at 60,000 Avios off-peak, and single-partner American Airlines domestic short-haul at 7,500-11,000 Avios. Once transferred, Avios move freely between BA, Iberia, Aer Lingus, Qatar and Finnair.

Transfer to Iberia Plus (not British Airways) if you're targeting Madrid transatlantic in off-peak — BA piles on fuel surcharges even on partner awards. Skip if you don't have specific US-Europe travel dates in mind: Avios balances aren't as flexible as they look.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Air Canada Aeroplan — 20% bonus[2][10]

Ends 30 September 2026 · 1,000:1,200 · Chase's only 2026 UR → airline bonus so far

Modest bonus on Chase's best airline partner. Aeroplan's Star Alliance chart is distance-and-region based with reasonable pricing (35,000 US→Europe economy, 60,000 in business off-peak), and Aeroplan has one of the strongest partner searches in the Star ecosystem. Aeroplan also allows one free stopover per one-way award for 5,000 additional miles, which few Star programs match.

Solid if you're already Aeroplan-fluent. If you're not, don't learn a new program for a 20% bonus — the learning curve is steeper than the discount is worth.

Citi ThankYou → LHW Leaders Club — 25% bonus[2]

Ends 19 September 2026 · Ratio 4:1 (instead of standard 5:1)

Leading Hotels of the World's Leaders Club is a niche destination — 5:1 base rate means 1,000 Citi points become 200 Leaders Club points normally, 250 with this bonus. Redemptions are typically 2,000-4,000 LHW points per night at participating luxury properties. Only makes sense if you have a specific LHW stay in mind at a property that participates in the Leaders Club redemption program — many don't.

Skip unless you already know which LHW property you're booking and have confirmed it accepts Leaders Club redemption points. The 4:1 rate is still a devaluation from historical norms — this is a "less bad" offer, not a good one.

Wyndham Rewards → Singapore KrisFlyer — 20% bonus[2]

A niche route for a niche user — Wyndham points come primarily from Wyndham co-brand card spend and hotel stays.
