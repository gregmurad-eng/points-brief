Today's Transfer Bonuses

Eight promotions still live across the seven major transferable-points programs. Six wrap between now and October 14, so this month is a compression window, not an open runway.

Amex Membership Rewards → British Airways · Iberia · Aer Lingus (Avios family)

30% bonus · transfer ratio 1,000 : 1,300 · expires September 27, 2026 · no minimum stated[1][2][8]

The routine semi-annual Amex Avios promo. Its most defensible use is Iberia short-haul to Madrid (11,000 Avios one-way in economy from JFK/BOS/ORD on off-peak dates) and BA single-partner AA short-haul at 7,500 Avios each way — routes that are still cash-cheap only when a Business Platinum holds Iberia's occasional flash sales.

Your move: transfer only against a specific held or on-hold award. The bonus runs 21 days; award space for Nov/Dec Iberia off-peak seats is what gates value, not the bonus itself. If you have no Avios redemption teed up, do nothing — Amex has run 25%+ Avios bonuses in five of the last six calendar years, and the next one is priced in.

Amex Membership Rewards → Hilton Honors

30% bonus · transfer ratio 1,000 : 2,600 (base 1:2 boosted) · expires October 14, 2026[1][2][8]

Hilton points are Amex's lowest-per-point hotel destination (~0.5¢), and even at 1:2.6 you are paying about 20,000 Amex points for a Hilton night that would normally list at 50,000 Honors. Only a ratio play in the narrow band where a Hilton property is genuinely cheaper in points than in cash you would otherwise spend.

Your move: skip unless you already have a Hilton stay booked in points and are topping off a specific balance. Transferring speculatively into Hilton has been a persistent regret for readers who later wanted those MR for a partner airline sweet spot.

Chase Ultimate Rewards → Air Canada Aeroplan

20% bonus (30% total for Chase Aeroplan cardholders, subject to a 25,000-bonus-point annual cap and a 50,000-point-per-transaction floor on the extra 10%) · effective ratio 1,000 : 1,200 (or 1,000 : 1,300 stacked) · started August 12, expires September 30, 2026 · 1,000 UR minimum[5][1]

The base bonus posts up to seven days after transfer, which matters if you are timing a booking. Aeroplan's dynamic partner pricing means the transfer only makes sense if you have already priced the target award — off-peak United transcons in narrow-body business, or Lufthansa First on partner space where availability is genuinely there.

Your move: transfer only if you have partner award space already held on an Aeroplan Manage Booking hold, or a saved United/ANA search showing the seat still open. Speculative transfers are the classic mistake here — Aeroplan's dynamic pricing can move on the same route within 24 hours.

Capital One Miles → Japan Airlines Mileage Bank

30% bonus on the base 1,000:750 ratio · effective 1,000 : 975 · expires September 30, 2026 · minimum transfer 1,000 miles[1][2][8]

Even bonused, this route still loses you 2.5% on the transfer — 1,000 Cap One miles becomes 975 JAL miles, not more. JAL's Oneworld partner chart is where the value lives (AA transcon business at 25,000 miles one-way, or Cathay Pacific first from JFK to HKG at 80,000 miles), not JAL-metal awards, which are Japan-heavy and often better redeemed via Alaska/Atmos.

Your move: transfer only against a specific JAL partner award already priced and held. This one erodes your Cap One balance unless the target award is priced 20%+ cheaper in JAL miles than in any alternate currency you also hold.

Citi ThankYou → Leaders Club (Leading Hotels of the World)

25% bonus · transfer ratio 1,000 : 250 (Strata Elite, Strata Premier, or Prestige cards only — other TY cards get less) · expires September 19, 2026[1][2][3]

A niche transfer. The Leaders Club program only redeems at LHW-affiliated luxury independents, and 1,000 TY → 250 LC points still lands you in a program where a night at a mid-tier LHW property typically costs 40,000–60,000 LC points. Practical utility only if you have a specific LHW stay booked and are trying to top off.

Your move: ignore unless you already have an LHW award booked and are short by a small increment. The bonus expires in 13 days but that timeline is only pressure if you have a use case — most readers do not.

Rove Miles → Copa Airlines ConnectMiles

40% launch bonus · effective ratio 1:1.4 · expires September 30, 2026 at 11:59pm EST · no announced minimum or cap[4][2]

Copa went live as a Rove transfer partner on September 1 with this launch bonus, and it is the most-elevated ratio in the current bonus board. Your move: only relevant if you already hold Rove Miles from card spend — which most readers do not. If you do, transfer only against a specific Copa-metal award you have priced. This launch bonus is our today's-play call above; see The Ledger for the follow-up prediction.

Program Watch

Flying Blue tiered fares launch in two days (Sept 8) — de-facto 25% Standard-fare devaluation on Air France and KLM direct awards

Starting Monday, September 8, 2026, Air France/KLM Flying Blue splits its direct-airline award tickets into three tiers: Light (no changes, refunds, seat selection, checked bag; no lounge access in business), Standard (~75,000 miles one-way in business, adds lounge and flexibility), and Flex (110,000 miles, reduces cash fees).[6][7] Flying Blue frames this as unchanged for partner-airline awards, which stay on the current partner chart — a distinction that matters, because the "de-facto 25% devaluation" applies only to AF/KLM direct metal, not to SkyTeam or Etihad/GOL/Kenya via Flying Blue.

Your move: if you were planning to book AF or KLM direct business class in Standard on miles you already hold, price the itinerary today — Sunday is the last day the old fare framework applies. Partner-airline pricing (Kenya, GOL, Etihad, SkyTeam) is unchanged and needs no urgent action.

Aeroplan Blackstone/La Caisse deal settled — no member-negative changes announced in the three weeks since

The Chase UR → Aeroplan 20% bonus running through September 30 is Air Canada's most visible current member commitment.

Your move: no action from this news — treat Aeroplan as stable for at least the near term. If you were holding off transferring UR to Aeroplan out of deal-panic, that hedge has not paid so far.
