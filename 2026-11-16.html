Program Watch

American Airlines × World of Hyatt: account-linking window closed yesterday

The 11:59 PM CT deadline on November 15 to link AAdvantage and World of Hyatt accounts has now passed. Members who linked before the deadline retain the ability to redeem AAdvantage miles for Hyatt Free Night Awards through February 28, 2027; after that date the partnership ends entirely except for a residual "500 AAdvantage miles per eligible Hyatt stay" option. Linked Hyatt elites can continue earning AAdvantage Loyalty Points through Milestone Rewards until December 31, 2026. Separately, Delta and Hyatt have confirmed their replacement partnership will include a true dual-earn (Delta SkyMiles and World of Hyatt points on the same eligible stay and flight), with earning rates still unannounced.[8][9]

Your move: if you missed the linking deadline, no further action is possible — the AA-Hyatt pathway is closed. If you linked before yesterday, use the next 15 months to burn any AAdvantage Loyalty Point Reward Hyatt certificates you've selected, since post-February 28, 2027 any unredeemed Hyatt awards obtained through AAdvantage become worthless.

Capital One × Qatar: one-year partnership milestone marked with an inaugural bonus

Capital One added Qatar Airways Privilege Club as a direct transfer partner in late 2025 and ran the full first year without a promo. The November 15 launch of the 15% bonus (above) functions as an anniversary gesture — a signal that Capital One treats Qatar as a kept partner, not a legacy addition. For Capital One-Qatar as a route, the structural news is that the Privilege Club's Avios combining mechanics give any Capital One cardholder a usable short-haul AAdvantage-via-BA pathway without holding a Chase or Amex card.[5][6]

Your move: no action on this news alone — but note the partnership is now an active promo channel, not a dormant one, which changes how you weigh Capital One miles against Chase and Amex for future Avios redemptions.

Delta SkyMiles: 2027 Medallion thresholds held unchanged for a third year

Delta confirmed that 2027 Medallion qualification stays at 5,000 MQD for Silver, 10,000 for Gold, 15,000 for Platinum, and 28,000 for Diamond — the third straight year without an increase. The contrast with United (which eliminated Premier Qualifying Flights entirely and raised Premier 1K to 32,000 PQP for 2027) is deliberate: Delta is positioning its program as the stable option at the moment its biggest rival just made status materially harder to earn. No award-pricing or transfer-partner changes attached to this announcement.[10]

Your move: no action unless you're running a Delta/United status comparison — if you are, the Delta-stability vs. United-spend-only divergence now matters more than any single bonus running today.
