Chase → World of Hyatt transfer ratio drops to 4:3 in 13 days (Oct 1, 2026)

Effective 2026-10-01 · Chase Sapphire Preferred + Ink Business Preferred existing cardholders · Chase Sapphire Reserve and Sapphire Reserve for Business retain 1:1[6][7][8]

The most consequential structural change in the transferable-points landscape this year. Announced with the June 15, 2026 Sapphire Preferred refresh: existing Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred cardholders drop from 1:1 to 4:3 (a 25% devaluation — 1,000 UR → 750 Hyatt points) on October 1. New applicants approved on or after June 15 already receive the 4:3 rate. Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Reserve for Business, Ink Preferred, Ink Plus and Corporate Flex are all affected too — Chase's own release lists them; only Sapphire Reserve products keep 1:1. Hyatt has consistently been the most valuable single Chase transfer destination at ~2¢/point; the new 4:3 rate roughly halves that arbitrage for CSP/Ink Business Preferred holders.

Today's Play

The Hyatt cliff — 12 days remain. If you hold a Chase Sapphire Preferred or Ink Business Preferred and you have a specific World of Hyatt redemption in mind (a Category 6 aspirational at 25k/night, a top-tier Park Hyatt, or fifth-night-free on a big stay), this is the last window to transfer at 1:1. Two hard rules make this a discipline test rather than a fire drill:

1. Do NOT transfer speculatively. Hyatt points cannot be transferred back to Chase, cannot be sold, and expire after 24 months of inactivity. A "just in case" stockpile at 1:1 is only a good idea if you already know the redemption; otherwise you have converted the most flexible currency in your stack into the least flexible.

2. If you hold Sapphire Reserve or Sapphire Reserve for Business, do NOTHING. Your 1:1 rate persists. The rush does not apply to you.

The reason today's Play is the Hyatt cliff and not the 70% Chase→Marriott bonus: the Hyatt change is a one-way structural devaluation on a durably valuable partner, while Chase→Marriott bonuses recur (though rarely at 70%). Optionality on Hyatt is what you lose forever on Oct 1; a Marriott bonus you can catch again.

before Sept 30, book (or place on 24-hour hold) the specific Hyatt award stay that would justify a transfer — then transfer only the amount needed for that stay, from CSP or Ink Business Preferred. Reserve cardholders: sit on your points.
